# Bachelorseminar SS 2026: M&A Prozess und organisatorische Integration — Vorbesprechung

**Introduction slides from the 29 April 2026 kick-off session of the Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt Bachelor seminar on M&A, covering all 8 paper topics, deadlines, grading rules, and scientific working methods — with Thema 7 ("Einfluss von AI auf M&A") as the user's assigned topic.**

## Key takeaways

- The seminar is structured around an integrative framework of M&A transaction types, processes, and organizational integration, taught by Prof. Dr. Lars Schweizer at Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt in SS 2026.
- Thema 7 — "Einfluss von AI auf M&A" — is one of two horizontal cross-cutting topics (alongside Thema 6: ESG) positioned as lenses applied across the entire M&A transaction lifecycle (due diligence, valuation, PMI, etc.).
- The hard submission deadline for both the written paper and the presentation slides is Tuesday, 2 June 2026 at 12:00; the seminar presentations occur on 8–9 June 2026, with Thema 7 scheduled for Tuesday 9 June, 10:00–11:00.
- The grade is 100% Seminararbeit (written paper); the oral presentation is a Studienleistung (ungraded pass/fail); minimum 80% attendance is required.
- Group size determines page count: 3-person groups write 20–25 text pages; 4-person groups write 25–30 pages; all groups append an Executive Summary of max. 3 pages integrated into the paper.
- A mandatory one-on-one group consultation (Pflicht-Besprechungstermin) must be scheduled via Moodle or email; the outline (Gliederung) must be submitted at least two days before that meeting.
- The seminar organizes its 8 topics across three layers: (I) M&A types/strategies, (II) the sequential deal process (due diligence → takeover tactics → synergy calculation → post-merger integration), and (III) M&A success measurement.

## Claims

- The seminar is titled "Mergers & Acquisitions: Prozess und organisatorische Integration" and is offered in SS 2026 at Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt am Main by Prof. Dr. Lars Schweizer.
- The Vorbesprechung (kick-off) took place on Wednesday, 29 April 2026, 17:00–20:00, room SH 3.105.
- There are 8 seminar paper topics in total; groups are 3–4 students (4 teams of 4 members and 4 teams of 3 members).
- Submission deadline for both Seminararbeit (.docx + .pdf) and Präsentation (.ppt + .pdf) is Tuesday, 2 June 2026 at 12:00.
- File naming convention: `BA_Seminar_Thema_[N]_Seminararbeit` and `BA_Seminar_Thema_[N]_Präsentation`.
- Seminar presentations run Monday 8 June (9:30–17:30) and Tuesday 9 June 2026 (9:00–13:30), room SH 3.105.
- Thema 7 presentation slot: Tuesday 9 June 2026, 10:00–11:00.
- Presentation duration: 25–30 minutes (3-person groups) or 30–35 minutes (4-person groups), followed by 15–20 minutes moderated discussion.
- Page length of written paper: 20–25 text pages (3-person group) or 25–30 text pages (4-person group), plus Executive Summary of max. 3 pages.

- A single mandatory consultation meeting per group must be arranged; the Gliederung (outline) is due at least two days before the meeting.
