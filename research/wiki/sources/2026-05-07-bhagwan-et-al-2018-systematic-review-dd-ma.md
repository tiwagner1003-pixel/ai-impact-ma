# A Systematic Review of the Due Diligence Stage of Mergers and Acquisitions

**Academic systematic review that maps the due diligence stage of M&A into areas, process factors, and principles, providing a stronger scholarly baseline for classical DD than practitioner manuals alone.**

## Key takeaways

- Due diligence is critical because it supplies information for negotiation parameters, bid prices, and integration recommendations.
- The paper identifies a research gap: there is no agreed guideline for which DD investigations to apply, how to prioritise them, what investigation depth is sufficient, and how DD outcomes should affect the transaction.
- The review categorises DD into three broad analytical buckets: DD areas, DD process factors, and DD concepts/principles.
- DD is explicitly multidisciplinary; the review identifies financial, legal, tax, environmental, operational, market, human resources, cultural, strategic, marketing, IP, technology, and R&D due diligence areas.
- Financial DD appears heavily weighted in the reviewed literature, while environmental, marketing, cultural, and IP DD also emerge as significant non-financial areas.
- The paper strengthens the seminar paper's claim that classical DD is broader than legal/financial document review, and that AI evidence concentrated in Legal DD covers only part of the full DD process.

## Claims

- Due diligence provides essential information used for negotiation parameters, bid prices, and integration recommendations. [[2026-05-07-bhagwan-et-al-2018-systematic-review-dd-ma]]
- A gap exists in research on which DD investigations should be applied to an M&A transaction, how they should be prioritised, and what level of investigation is needed to minimise risk. [[2026-05-07-bhagwan-et-al-2018-systematic-review-dd-ma]]
- Traditional DD typically reviews "hard data" and focuses heavily on legal and financial issues, but a thorough DD should consider all aspects of the target's business. [[2026-05-07-bhagwan-et-al-2018-systematic-review-dd-ma]]
- DD's role in M&A is to support valuation, arm negotiators, test representations and warranties, fulfil disclosure requirements, and inform post-merger integration planning. [[2026-05-07-bhagwan-et-al-2018-systematic-review-dd-ma]]
- Factors impeding DD depth include time restrictions, cost constraints, and situational factors. [[2026-05-07-bhagwan-et-al-2018-systematic-review-dd-ma]]
- The authors identify 13 DD areas: financial, legal, tax, environmental, operational, market, human resources, cultural, strategic, marketing, intellectual property, technology, and R&D. [[2026-05-07-bhagwan-et-al-2018-systematic-review-dd-ma]]
- The highest number of extracted concepts/factors in the review concern DD process steps and risk factors, reinforcing that DD quality is a process-governance problem, not merely a document-analysis problem. [[2026-05-07-bhagwan-et-al-2018-systematic-review-dd-ma]]
