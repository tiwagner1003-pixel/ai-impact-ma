# Measuring Corporate Culture Using Machine Learning

**Li et al. (2021) use word embeddings and earnings-call transcripts to construct large-sample measures of corporate culture and link culture to firm outcomes, including deal making.**

## Key takeaways

- The paper builds a culture dictionary using word embeddings and 209,480 earnings-call transcripts.
- It scores five cultural values: innovation, integrity, quality, respect, and teamwork.
- The dataset covers 62,664 firm-year observations from 2001 to 2018.
- Corporate culture correlates with operational efficiency, risk-taking, earnings management, compensation design, firm value, and deal making.
- The paper provides a broader methodological foundation for ML-based culture measurement, complementing the M&A-specific Culture-BERT evidence from Brede et al. (2025).

## Claims

- Machine learning can operationalize otherwise nebulous corporate-culture constructs at scale using text data. [[2026-05-05-li-et-al-2021-corporate-culture-ml]]
- Corporate culture is associated with multiple business outcomes, including deal making. [[2026-05-05-li-et-al-2021-corporate-culture-ml]]
- Major corporate events such as mergers and acquisitions can shape corporate culture. [[2026-05-05-li-et-al-2021-corporate-culture-ml]]
