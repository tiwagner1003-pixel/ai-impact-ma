# Bachelorseminar M&A SS 2026 — Syllabus mit Literaturliste

**The full syllabus for the SS 2026 Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt Bachelor seminar on M&A, containing the complete topic list with official titles, per-topic reading lists, grading rules, submission requirements, and — critically for the user — the four prescribed readings for Thema 7 ("Der Einfluss von KI auf M&A").**

## Key takeaways

- Thema 7 is officially titled "Der Einfluss von KI auf M&A" and has four prescribed starter readings covering AI activity forecasting (LLMs), ML-driven target selection and synergy prediction, and a legal-transformative perspective on AI in deal processes.
- The syllabus confirms and clarifies correct topic titles for all 8 themes; the intro slides used slightly different formulations (e.g., Thema 3 is "Takeover-Taktiken und Abwehrtechniken," not "Due Diligence und Takeover-Taktiken").
- FTI Andersch is named as the Praxispartner (industry guest) for seminar Day 1 (8 June 2026), providing both a keynote lecture and discussion partnership for all presentations that day.
- The grading structure is confirmed: 100% Seminararbeit (Prüfungsleistung); presentation is Studienleistung (pass/fail only); 80% attendance required.
- Prof. Schweizer appears as co-author on readings assigned to other topics (Thema 4: Knyphausen-Auseß, Köppen & Schweizer 2007; Thema 5: Schweizer & Patzelt 2012; Schweizer 2005), revealing his own research profile in synergy identification and post-merger integration of biotech firms.
- The seminar reading lists span eight distinct research areas, each with 5–12 academic sources, providing a mapped bibliography for the full M&A field at Bachelor level.
- Paper length requirements are confirmed: 20–25 pages for 3-person groups, 25–30 pages for 4-person groups; all groups prepend a 3-page Executive Summary.

## Claims

- The official German title of Thema 7 is "Der Einfluss von KI auf M&A" (not "Einfluss von AI auf M&A" as shown in the intro slides).
- The four prescribed readings for Thema 7 are: Bremen (2024, WTW online article), Degen et al. (2024, academic paper on LLMs and M&A activity forecasting using ChatGPT), Singh (2022, Jus Corpus law journal), and Zheng & Lin Li (2024, JAIGS — ML-based target selection and synergy prediction).
- Thema 1 is "M&A-Strategien und theoretische Erklarungsansatze" with 7 assigned readings.
- Thema 2 is "M&A Prozess, Organisation und beteiligte Player" with 7 assigned readings.
- Thema 3 is "Takeover-Taktiken und Abwehrtechniken" with 6 assigned readings (recommended films: Wall Street, Barbarians at the Gate).
- Thema 4 is "Synergie-Berechnung und Bewertung" with 9 assigned readings, including Schweizer as co-author (Knyphausen-Auseß et al. 2007).
- Thema 5 is "Post-Merger-Integrations-Strategien/-Ansatze und kulturelle Integration" with 12 assigned readings, including two papers by Schweizer.
- Thema 6 is "Die Bedeutung von ESG in M&A" with 5 assigned readings.
- Thema 8 is "Messung von M&A Erfolg" with 13 assigned readings, the largest reading list in the seminar.
- FTI Andersch is named as Praxispartner for seminar Day 1 (Monday 8 June 2026), providing a Gastvortrag (keynote) and serving as Diskussionspartner for presentations on that day.
- The paper submission deadline is Tuesday, 2 June 2026 at 12:00 for PDF (Moodle), Word version (email to junge@marketing.uni-frankfurt.de and Hochhaus@econ.uni-frankfurt.de), and a printed version (RuW 3.207 or Hauspostfach 50).
- Presentation slides are also due 2 June 2026 at 12:00 as PDF (Moodle) and PPT (email); two printed copies of slides must be submitted to the chair.
- In-text citation style is used (not footnotes): format "(Author Year, p. X)" within the running text.
- Attendance requirement: minimum 80% of seminar time.
- The Ehrenwortliche Erklarung (honor statement) must be appended to every submitted paper.
- Three general introductory references span all topics: Devers et al. (2020, AoM Annals), Haleblian et al. (2009, Journal of Management), and Hettich (2014, M&A REVIEW).
- A comprehensive textbook reading list (Semesterapparat) includes 18 books available in the RuW library and online via Goethe-Universitat library and Business Source Premier.

## Notes

### Thema 7 reading annotations

**Bremen, John M. (2024): "AI accelerates M&A into the future."** WTW (Willis Towers Watson) practitioner article, 26 June 2024. URL: https://www.wtwco.com/en-cm/insights/2024/06/ai-accelerates-m-and-a-into-the-future. A consulting/industry perspective on AI adoption in M&A; likely covers deal sourcing, due diligence automation, and integration analytics.

**Degen, D.; Kengelbach, J.; Kim, D.; Sievers, S.; Wang, Y. (2024): "Large Language Models and M&A: Can ChatGPT help forecast M&A activity?"** Academic working paper (appears to be BCG-affiliated). Focuses specifically on LLM capabilities for predicting M&A deal activity — a quantitative/empirical angle. This is the most technically specific Thema 7 reading.

**Singh, Kavya Sanjay (2022): "The Transformative Potential of Artificial Intelligence and Its Impact on M&A Transactions."** Jus Corpus Law Journal, Vol. 3, p. 249. URL: https://heinonline.org/HOL/Page?handle=hein.journals/juscrp3&id=6855. Legal/regulatory perspective on AI in M&A. Covers transformation potential broadly.

**Zheng; Lin Li (2024): "AI-Driven M&A Target Selection and Synergy Prediction: A Machine Learning-Based Approach."** JAIGS, Vol. 6(1), pp. 359–377. DOI: 10.60087/jaigs.v6i1.260. The most operationally specific paper: ML for both target identification and synergy estimation — directly relevant to Thema 4 connections.

### Schweizer's own research (relevant for framing)

- Knyphausen-Auseß, Köppen & Schweizer (2007): "Identifying synergies ahead of mergers and acquisitions," IJFSM Vol. 2 No. 4. Shows Schweizer researches pre-deal synergy identification — relevant theoretical framing for how AI improves this process.
- Schweizer & Patzelt (2012): Employee commitment in post-acquisition integration — leadership style and integration speed. Schweizer's PMI research focuses on human/organizational factors; AI's role in this domain is less established.
- Schweizer (2005): Organizational integration of acquired biotech companies in pharma — hybrid strategy. Domain-specific integration research.

### Topic title correction vs. prior source

The intro slides (2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro) listed Thema 3 as "Due Diligence und Takeover-Taktiken." The authoritative syllabus shows Thema 3 is "Takeover-Taktiken und Abwehrtechniken" and that due diligence is embedded in Thema 2 ("M&A Prozess, Organisation und beteiligte Player") rather than having its own topic. This changes the structural interpretation of the framework somewhat.

### Key textbooks for full M&A coverage

For a cross-cutting paper on AI in M&A, the most useful textbooks from the Semesterapparat are:
- DePamphilis (2003) — comprehensive process textbook, all chapters
- Glaum & Hutschenreuter (2010) — German-language comprehensive reference, chapters assigned per topic
- Faulkner, Teerikangas & Joseph (2012), The Handbook of Mergers and Acquisitions — Oxford handbook, chapters assigned to multiple topics
- Sirower (1997) and Sirower & Weirens (2022) — synergy trap and synergy solution, assigned to Thema 4
