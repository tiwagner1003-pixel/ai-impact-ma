# Taking Stock of What We Know About Mergers and Acquisitions

**Haleblian et al. (2009) provide a broad review and organizing framework for M&A research across management, economics, finance, accounting, and sociology.**

## Key takeaways

- The paper consolidates fragmented M&A research and develops a framework for acquisition antecedents, moderators, performance outcomes, and research gaps.
- Early finance research generally finds that acquisitions do not enhance acquirer value, while target shareholders usually gain.
- M&A research spans multiple disciplines, but theoretical integration remains limited.
- The review is a strong anchor for the M&A foundation section because it gives the broad academic landscape without being tied to AI.

## Claims

- Acquisition research shows practical importance but lacks sufficient theoretical integration across disciplines. [[2026-05-05-haleblian-et-al-2009-ma-review]]
- Early finance studies often found neutral or negative acquirer returns, while target shareholders generally benefit from acquisition premiums. [[2026-05-05-haleblian-et-al-2009-ma-review]]
- More recent M&A research focuses on antecedents, moderators, and conditions under which acquisitions benefit acquirers. [[2026-05-05-haleblian-et-al-2009-ma-review]]
