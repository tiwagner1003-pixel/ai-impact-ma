# Putting a human in the loop

**PLOS ONE experiment showing that human-in-the-loop designs can increase uptake of algorithmic recommendations but may reduce decision accuracy because humans over-rely on algorithmic advice and fail to correct the worst recommendations.**

## Key takeaways

- The study directly challenges the simple governance claim that "human-in-the-loop" automatically improves AI decision quality.
- In an online experiment (N=292), participants chose recommendations from an algorithm or another human in a prediction task.
- Participants preferred algorithmic recommendations 66% of the time over equally accurate human recommendations.
- Allowing participants to monitor and adjust recommendations increased preference for the algorithm by 7 percentage points.
- Participants followed algorithmic recommendations more closely and were less likely to intervene when recommendations were least accurate.
- For the DD paper, this is a strong source for Automation Bias and for qualifying Human-in-the-Loop as a necessary but insufficient governance mechanism.

## Claims

- Human oversight is often proposed as a legal and governance safeguard for automated decision-making, including in GDPR Article 22 and AI Act discussions. [[2026-05-07-sele-chugunova-2024-human-in-loop-adm]]
- Participants preferred delegating to an algorithm over an equally accurate human in 66% of cases. [[2026-05-07-sele-chugunova-2024-human-in-loop-adm]]
- Human-in-the-loop design increased algorithm uptake by 7 percentage points because users retained some agency over the decision. [[2026-05-07-sele-chugunova-2024-human-in-loop-adm]]
- Participants followed algorithmic recommendations more closely than human recommendations, despite equal average quality. [[2026-05-07-sele-chugunova-2024-human-in-loop-adm]]
- Participants were less likely to intervene with the least accurate recommendations, weakening the intended quality-control function of human oversight. [[2026-05-07-sele-chugunova-2024-human-in-loop-adm]]
- In this experiment, human-in-the-loop increased uptake but decreased final decision accuracy. [[2026-05-07-sele-chugunova-2024-human-in-loop-adm]]
