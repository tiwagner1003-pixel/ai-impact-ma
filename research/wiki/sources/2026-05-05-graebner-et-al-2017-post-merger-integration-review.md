# The Process of Post-Merger Integration

**Graebner et al. (2017) review the fragmented PMI literature and organize it around strategic integration, sociocultural integration, and experience and learning.**

## Key takeaways

- PMI plays a critical role in M&A success but remains theoretically fragmented.
- The review organizes PMI into strategic integration, sociocultural integration, and experience/learning.
- The authors call for more processual research on temporality, decision-making, practices/tools, and emotionality.
- The source is especially useful for framing why AI in PMI should be treated cautiously: integration is not only an information-processing task but also a social and temporal process.

## Claims

- M&A transactions remain common despite often disappointing outcomes, making PMI a central explanatory area for M&A success and failure. [[2026-05-05-graebner-et-al-2017-post-merger-integration-review]]
- PMI research should attend to strategic integration, sociocultural integration, and acquisition experience/learning. [[2026-05-05-graebner-et-al-2017-post-merger-integration-review]]
- A process view is important because integration unfolds over time through decisions, practices, emotional reactions, and social dynamics. [[2026-05-05-graebner-et-al-2017-post-merger-integration-review]]

## Notes

This source should temper claims that AI can "solve" PMI. It supports the argument that AI can assist with information and coordination, while cultural and emotional integration require human managerial work.
