# Harvard Citation Style

**An author-date in-text citation system in which short references are embedded directly in the running text as `(Author Year, page)`, with complete bibliographic information in a reference list at the end.**

## Summary

The Harvard style is the mandatory citation format at the Lehrstuhl für BWL, insb. Strategisches Management at Goethe-Universität Frankfurt. In-text references appear as parenthetical short citations immediately following the cited material. The full reference is given once in the Literaturverzeichnis (bibliography). [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

Two citation forms are distinguished: direct quotations (direktes/wörtliches Zitat) reproduce a source verbatim, are enclosed in quotation marks, and cite as `(Author Year, page)`; indirect quotations (indirektes/sinngemäßes Zitat) paraphrase a source and cite as `(vgl. Author Year, page)`. Secondary citations (quoting a source you know only through another source) should be used only as an exception and are formatted as `(zit. nach ...)`. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

Plagiarism (Verstoß gegen die Zitierpflicht) inevitably results in a grade of "mangelhaft." Every borrowed idea — whether quoted verbatim or paraphrased — must be cited. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

## Reference list formats

### Book (single author)
`Familienname, Vorname (Jahr): "Titel, ggf. Untertitel", [Aufl.], Ort: Verlag.`

### Book (two or more authors)
`Familienname, Vorname / Familienname, Vorname (Jahr): "Titel", Ort: Verlag.`

### Chapter in edited volume
`Familienname, Vorname (Jahr): "Titel des Beitrags", in: Familienname, Vorname (Hrsg.): "Titel Sammelwerk", Ort: Verlag, S. xx-yy.`

### Journal article
`Familienname, Vorname (Jahr): "Titel des Aufsatzes", in: Name der Zeitschrift, Jg. (Band) (Jahr), Heft-Nr., S. xx-yy.`

### Internet source
`Familienname, Vorname (Datum): "Titel", in: URL, (gesichtet am: Datum).`

## Key claims across sources

- The chair mandates Harvard in-text citation style with short references `(Author Jahr, Seitenangabe)` directly in the body text. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Lecture slides (Vorlesungsskripte) are not valid citable sources. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Unpublished, non-public sources (e.g., company statistics, interviews) must be submitted as an appendix to become citable. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Typical citation errors: missing references, too few or unsuitable sources, incorrect paraphrasing, a chain of citations without own argumentation, inconsistent application of the citation style. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- The goal is to argue with the literature, not merely to accumulate citations. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

## AI citation rules

AI-generated content requires disclosure beyond the standard bibliography [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]:

- AI-assisted text passages, visualizations, data generation, data transformations, and other substantive contributions must be marked in-text or in footnotes.
- Idea-finding uses of AI must also be disclosed.
- A separate **KI-Verzeichnis** (AI-tools index) is required alongside the bibliography, with columns: KI-basiertes Hilfsmittel + Version | Einsatzform | Betroffene Teile der Arbeit | Bemerkungen.
- AI-generated content is not a scientific source and cannot substitute for academic literature.
- AI frequently fabricates bibliographic references ("erfindet oft Literaturangaben") — all secondary sources must be independently verified.

Tools **exempt** from the KI-Verzeichnis: Microsoft Office, LaTeX, OpenOffice, iWork, Google Scholar, university library databases, reference managers (Zotero, Endnote, Mendeley, Citavi), spell/grammar checkers, online dictionaries, R, Python, SPSS.
