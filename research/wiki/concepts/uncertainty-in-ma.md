# Uncertainty in M&A

**The set of information, valuation, macro-policy, interim, and governance uncertainties that shape whether M&A deals are initiated, priced, completed, and ultimately create value.**

## Summary


For the AI-in-M&A research question, uncertainty is useful as an analytical lens rather than a narrow standalone topic. AI may reduce information and valuation uncertainty through target screening, due diligence, sentiment extraction, and deal-success prediction, but it can also create model, explainability, governance, and liability uncertainty when outputs are opaque or wrong. [[2026-05-04-zhang-et-al-2025-digital-transformation-ma-information-asymmetry]] [[2026-05-04-bozman-et-al-2026-ai-deal-screening]] [[2026-05-04-herbosch-mertens-2025-risk-allocation-ai-ma]]

## Variations / sub-concepts

- Information uncertainty — incomplete knowledge about target quality, value, risks, and synergies.
- Policy uncertainty — uncertainty about regulatory, fiscal, monetary, tax, and government spending policies.
- Interim uncertainty — uncertainty between announcement and closing, especially for public-target deals.
- Valuation uncertainty — uncertainty about standalone value, synergies, and investor reactions.
- Governance and model uncertainty — uncertainty created by opaque or erroneous AI outputs and unclear responsibility.

## Key claims across sources

- Digital transformation promotes M&A primarily by reducing external information asymmetry between acquirers and targets. [[2026-05-04-zhang-et-al-2025-digital-transformation-ma-information-asymmetry]]
- AI deal-screening models can reduce uncertainty about deal success by forecasting announcement returns and identifying downside-risk deals, but their effectiveness is lower for complex deals. [[2026-05-04-bozman-et-al-2026-ai-deal-screening]]
- AI-guided transactions create new legal and governance risks because AI systems are imperfect and boards cannot blindly rely on unexplainable outputs for economically significant M&A decisions. [[2026-05-04-herbosch-mertens-2025-risk-allocation-ai-ma]]
