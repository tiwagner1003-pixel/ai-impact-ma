# Corporate Culture

**A firm's shared values and behavioral norms, increasingly measurable through machine-learning analysis of organizational text.**

## Summary

Li et al. (2021) operationalize corporate culture using word embeddings and earnings-call transcripts, scoring innovation, integrity, quality, respect, and teamwork across a large firm-year panel. [[2026-05-05-li-et-al-2021-corporate-culture-ml]]

In the M&A context, Brede et al. (2025) and Li et al. (2021) jointly support the idea that culture can be measured from text and linked to deal outcomes or deal-related behavior. [[2026-05-04-brede-et-al-2025-cultural-distance-ma-glassdoor]] [[2026-05-05-li-et-al-2021-corporate-culture-ml]]

## Key claims across sources

- Machine learning can turn unstructured organizational language into quantitative culture measures. [[2026-05-05-li-et-al-2021-corporate-culture-ml]]
- Corporate culture is associated with business outcomes, including deal making. [[2026-05-05-li-et-al-2021-corporate-culture-ml]]
- Cultural distance between acquirer and target predicts worse M&A outcomes in Brede et al. (2025). [[2026-05-04-brede-et-al-2025-cultural-distance-ma-glassdoor]]
