# Pecking Order Theory

**Corporate-finance logic that firms prefer internal funds, then debt, and issue equity last because asymmetric information makes equity issuance costly.**

## Summary

Myers & Majluf (1984) show that when managers have private information, issuing equity can send a negative signal to investors and may cause firms to forgo positive-NPV projects. [[2026-05-05-myers-majluf-1984-corporate-financing-investment-decisions]]

For the M&A seminar paper, this theory is useful mainly as a corporate-finance extension of information asymmetry, not as a central AI-in-M&A source. [[2026-05-05-myers-majluf-1984-corporate-financing-investment-decisions]]
