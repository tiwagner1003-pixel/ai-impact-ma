# Scientific Writing (Wissenschaftliches Arbeiten)

**The systematic and traceable pursuit of knowledge through verifiable argumentation, methodical procedure, and critical engagement with existing research.**

## Summary

Scientific writing (Wissenschaftliches Arbeiten) is distinguished from non-scientific writing by four core characteristics: methodical and rational procedure, an open-ended process (ergebnisoffener Prozess), the aim of knowledge creation (Wissensmehrung), and orientation toward the existing state of research. Every claim must be grounded in verifiable sources; opinions, unsubstantiated assertions ("man weiß", "es ist offensichtlich"), and selective source use are markers of non-scientific work. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

The goal of scientific writing is not to persuade or entertain but to generate and communicate knowledge in a reproducible, traceable way. The canonical contrast is: an unsupported claim ("Digitale Transformation ist wichtig") vs. a properly framed, literature-backed statement with an in-text citation. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

At the chair for Strategisches Management at Goethe-Universität Frankfurt, quality is assessed as a function of clarity and structure, not length: "Qualität entsteht durch Klarheit und Struktur, nicht durch Umfang." A good paper has a clear research question, logical argumentation, sound literature work, and clean presentation. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

## Key claims across sources

- Scientific writing is defined as systematic and traceable knowledge-seeking (systematische und nachvollziehbare Erkenntnissuche). [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Non-scientific work is characterized by personal opinions, unsubstantiated claims, and unsystematic source selection. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Quality arises from clarity and structure, not from length. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- A correct paper structure follows Einleitung → Grundlagenteil → Hauptteil → Schlussteil; but the recommended writing order is Grundlagenteil first, Einleitung and Schlussteil last. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

## Paper structure requirements (Goethe chair)

| Section | Core function | Typical errors |
|---|---|---|
| Einleitung | Introduce topic, state research question, outline structure | Definitions, tables, results; no clear Fragestellung |
| Grundlagenteil | Theoretical context, define terms, survey literature | Pure summary without link to Fragestellung; inconsistent terminology |
| Hauptteil | Structured analysis, systematic argument, critical engagement | Description instead of analysis; no red thread; missing critical appraisal |
| Schlussteil | Summarize results, tie back to Fragestellung, end with Kernaussage | New content introduced; pure repetition; weak closing statement |

## Formatting requirements (Goethe chair)

- Paper length: 25–30 Textseiten including figures [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Font: Arial or Times New Roman, 12 pt body / 10 pt footnotes [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Line spacing: 1.5 in body text; single in footnotes [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Alignment: block justification (Blocksatz) with automatic hyphenation [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Margins: left 4 cm, right 2 cm, top 2.5 cm, bottom 3 cm (apply to body, footnotes, lists, and appendix) [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Use the provided template [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

## Grading criteria (Goethe chair)

Seven assessed dimensions [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]:

1. **Behandlung der Fragestellung** — no off-topic digressions; all sub-questions addressed; argument chains logically consistent
2. **Ergebnisse** — clearly stated, internally consistent, and traceable from the argument
3. **Definitionen und Prämissen** — key terms defined and used consistently; assumptions made explicit
4. **Eigenständigkeit** — independent thinking, recognition of literature gaps, engagement with contradictions
5. **Sprache und Stil** — clear, precise, appropriately academic language
6. **Struktur und Darstellung** — logical outline, consistent headings, well-placed paragraphs
7. **Formale Anforderungen** — correct and consistent citation, compliance with formatting rules, error-free presentation
