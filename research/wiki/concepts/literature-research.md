# Literature Research (Literaturrecherche)

**The systematic process of identifying, evaluating, and curating scholarly sources to establish the state of research on a topic.**

## Summary

Literature research is the evidence-gathering foundation of scientific writing. Its goals are to systematically capture the current state of research on a topic and to identify the central concepts, results, and scholarly positions. The starting point is always a concrete research question, not an open-ended browsing session. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

Effective literature research combines multiple access routes: subject databases (JSTOR, WISO, EconLit), citation databases (Web of Science, Scopus), general academic search (Google Scholar), and the snowball method for depth. No single database is sufficient on its own. The quality of the literature research directly determines the quality of the paper. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

Source selection criteria include: fit with the research question, publication in a peer-reviewed journal, number and quality of citations, and recency. Abstracts should be used for rapid pre-selection; not every paper needs to be read in full — targeted reading of introduction and conclusion sections is standard practice. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

## Literature types

| Type | Examples | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Überblicksliteratur | Textbooks, handbooks, review articles | Entry point and orientation |
| Vertiefende Forschungsliteratur | Journal articles, working papers | Central scientific basis |
| Ergänzende Literatur | Edited volumes (Sammelbände), specialist books | Context and depth |

## Typical workflow

1. Start with Google Scholar or overview literature
2. Search subject databases (JSTOR, WISO, EconLit)
3. Select relevant articles (abstract, journal reputation, citation count)
4. Deepen via snowball method
5. Build a structured reference list

Combination of different search routes is decisive. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

## Key claims across sources

- Literature research quality determines paper quality: "Qualität der Recherche bestimmt die Qualität der Arbeit." [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Textbooks are not sufficient as the primary source base; peer-reviewed journal articles are the central foundation. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- AI tools can support literature research for initial orientation, developing search terms, structuring sub-questions, and reflecting on search strategy — but cannot replace databases and specialist literature. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- AI-generated source references must be verified independently; AI frequently fabricates citations. [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]

## Dos and Don'ts

**Dos:** targeted database searches; combining multiple search routes; using current, relevant journal articles; abstracts for pre-selection; reading targeted sections (introduction, conclusion).

**Don'ts:** only using Google or only the first results page; unsystematic or selective source selection; focusing on textbooks instead of research literature; unverified adoption of AI-generated sources.
