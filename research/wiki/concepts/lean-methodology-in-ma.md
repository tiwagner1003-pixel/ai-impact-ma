# Lean Methodology in M&A

**The application of lean manufacturing principles — originating with the Toyota Production System — to M&A processes in order to reduce waste, cut unnecessary costs, and improve due diligence and integration efficiency.**

## Summary

Lean methodology originated in manufacturing (notably the Toyota Production System) as a "just in time" strategy for delivering the right parts at the right time, minimising excess inventory and resource waste. Singh (2023) presents it as a process improvement framework applicable to M&A due diligence and integration, where traditional approaches are characterised as demanding, time-consuming, and expensive. [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]

The five-stage lean implementation cycle — value definition, value stream mapping, flow creation, pull-based demand, and iterative lean transformation — is adapted to M&A by: (1) mapping the current transaction process and its failure points, (2) identifying value flows from the client's perspective, (3) removing functional barriers, and (4) repeating the cycle until the process output aligns with client needs. [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]

Singh (2023) also cites Stefan Rao Kostas (2022, Tennessee Journal of Business Law) as arguing that lean processes and AI together can remedy the pitfalls of traditional M&A — framing lean and AI as complementary rather than competing methodologies. The needless costs in the current deal-closing-focused M&A approach are described as making up "a double-digit percentage of total costs." [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]

## Key claims across sources

- The current M&A transaction approach is primarily focused on deal closing; needless additional costs make up a double-digit percentage of total costs, motivating lean approaches. [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]
- The lean technique reduces the need for additional resources and costs by using a "just in time" strategy — a methodology frequently used in the automotive industry and now adapted to M&A. [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]
- By mapping and analysing prior transaction failures, transaction teams can apply lean principles to uncover improvements and learn from their mistakes. [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]
- Lean and AI are presented as complementary frameworks: lean addresses process structure and waste reduction; AI addresses data processing capacity and analytical depth. [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]
