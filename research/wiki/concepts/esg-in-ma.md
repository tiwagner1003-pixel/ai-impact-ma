# ESG in M&A (Einfluss von ESG auf M&A)

**The study of how Environmental, Social, and Governance factors influence M&A strategy, deal screening, due diligence, valuation, and post-merger integration.**

## Summary

ESG in M&A is Thema 6 of the Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt Bachelor seminar SS 2026. Like Thema 7 (AI in M&A), it is positioned as a horizontal cross-cutting topic spanning the entire M&A process rather than a single deal phase. [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]

ESG considerations increasingly affect target selection, due diligence scope, valuation adjustments (ESG risk premia), and integration priorities. Thema 6 presents on Monday 8 June 2026, in the 9:00–10:00 slot. [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]

## Key claims across sources

- ESG is positioned in the seminar framework as a cross-cutting influence equivalent in scope to AI, both spanning the full deal process. [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]
- The five prescribed readings for Thema 6 cover: sustainability's role in M&A (Ahammad 2023), cultural similarity and CSR effects on M&A (Bereskin et al. 2018), CSR and M&A performance in Europe (Brunner-Kirchmair & Wagner 2024), CSR and abnormal returns in M&A events (Chao & Ho 2019), and a systematic literature review on sustainability across deal stages (Kayser & Zulch 2024). [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-syllabus]]
- Galpin & Vibe (2024) in Strategy & Leadership provides a practitioner framework for incorporating ESG across all stages of the M&A process — the practitioner counterpart to the academic readings. [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-syllabus]]
