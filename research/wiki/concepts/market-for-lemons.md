# Market for Lemons

**A mechanism where buyers' inability to observe quality before purchase causes adverse selection, lowering average market quality and potentially shrinking or destroying the market.**

## Summary

Akerlof (1970) uses the used-car market to show how asymmetric information can cause high-quality sellers to exit when buyers price goods based on average expected quality. [[2026-05-05-akerlof-1970-market-for-lemons]]

In M&A, the logic is useful by analogy: acquirers often cannot fully observe target quality before closing, so due diligence and information-processing tools exist to reduce adverse-selection risk. [[2026-05-05-akerlof-1970-market-for-lemons]]
