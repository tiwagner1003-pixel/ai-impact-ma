# Clifford Chance

**One of the world's largest international law firms, headquartered in London; operates an internal Data Science Lab that co-authored the first NLP/LLM study on M&A due diligence (NAACL 2024).**

## Overview

Clifford Chance is a Magic Circle law firm with global operations across major financial centres. In the context of the wiki, Clifford Chance is relevant as the industry partner in the Jang & Stikkel (2024) study — the firm's Data Science Lab (represented by [[gabor-stikkel]]) collaborated with the University of Oxford Department of Computer Science to apply NLP and LLM techniques to the M&A due diligence problem. [[2026-05-04-jang-stikkel-2024-nlp-llms-due-diligence]]

The existence of a dedicated Data Science Lab within Clifford Chance signals that leading law firms are investing in proprietary AI capabilities for legal work including M&A due diligence — providing real-world grounding for the study's findings and recommendations. [[2026-05-04-jang-stikkel-2024-nlp-llms-due-diligence]]
