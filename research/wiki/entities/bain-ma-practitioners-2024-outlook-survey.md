# Bain M&A Practitioners' 2024 Outlook Survey

**Bain & Company's 2024 survey of approximately 306 M&A practitioners on their use of and attitudes toward generative AI in M&A deal processes; the primary empirical data source for Bain's "Generative AI in M&A: Where Hope Meets Hype" article.**

## Overview

The Bain M&A Practitioners' 2024 Outlook Survey is the empirical backbone of the January 2024 Bain article on generative AI in M&A. It surveyed approximately 306 M&A practitioners and distinguishes between current users (N=50) and non-users (N=256). The survey is the direct source of the widely-cited figures: 16% current generative AI adoption and 80% expected adoption within three years. [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]]

Note: The Bremen (2024) article — another prescribed Thema 7 reading already in the wiki — cites these same figures (16%/80%) and attributes them to a "BCG survey." This is likely a mis-attribution: the figures originate in this Bain survey. A conflict callout exists on [[generative-ai-in-ma]] and [[boston-consulting-group]].

## Key facts

- Total respondents: approximately 306 M&A practitioners. [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]]
- Current generative AI users subsample: N=50. [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]]
- Non-users subsample: N=256. [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]]
- Survey conducted and published in early 2024; article published January 2024. [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]]
- Detailed sampling methodology, geographic coverage, and company size distribution are not published in the article.
