# Adair Morse

**Finance professor at UC Berkeley Haas School of Business and researcher at the U.S. Department of the Treasury; co-author of Bartlett et al. (2022) on FinTech lending discrimination.**

## Overview

Adair Morse holds a dual affiliation at the Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley, and the U.S. Department of the Treasury. She is co-author of Bartlett, Morse, Stanton & Wallace (2022), published in the Journal of Financial Economics, providing empirical evidence that minority borrowers are charged 4–5 basis points more than equally creditworthy non-minority borrowers by both FinTech and non-FinTech lenders. [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]

Her research focuses on household finance, consumer credit, fintech, and financial regulation.
