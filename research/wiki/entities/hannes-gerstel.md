# Hannes Gerstel

**Management researcher at Friedrich Schiller University Jena (Germany); co-author of Brede et al. (2025) on organizational cultural distance and M&A outcomes.**

## Overview

Hannes Gerstel is affiliated with Friedrich Schiller University Jena and is the second author of "Mind the gap: the effect of cultural distance on mergers and acquisitions — evidence from Glassdoor reviews," published in the *Review of Managerial Science* (Vol. 19, 2025).
