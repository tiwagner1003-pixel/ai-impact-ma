# City University London

**Public research university in London; institutional affiliation of Kate Goddard and Abdul Roudsari on the 2012 automation bias systematic review.**

## Overview

City University London (now City, University of London) is a public research university in London, UK. The Centre for Health Informatics at City University was the primary institutional home of Kate Goddard and Abdul Roudsari during the 2012 automation bias systematic review.
