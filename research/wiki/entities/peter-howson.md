# Peter Howson

**M&A and commercial due diligence practitioner; director at AMR International; author of the standard reference textbook on the classical DD process.**

## Overview

Peter Howson (born 1957) is a director at [[amr-international]], London's leading independent commercial due diligence specialist. He has over 25 years of M&A and business development experience in both industry and as an adviser, spanning private equity and corporate transactions, domestic and cross-border deals, in manufacturing and service industries. [[2026-05-07-howson-2003-due-diligence-critical-stage]]

He is a regular speaker at M&A and due diligence conferences. His principal intellectual contribution is the argument that due diligence is fundamentally an exercise in project management — not primarily a legal or accounting exercise — and that it must span all major business functions and be explicitly connected to post-acquisition integration planning. [[2026-05-07-howson-2003-due-diligence-critical-stage]]
