# David J. Ketchen Jr.

**Strategy and management professor at Auburn University and co-author of the Bergh et al. (2019) systematic review of information asymmetry in management research.**

## Overview

David J. Ketchen Jr. is a prolific strategy researcher at Auburn University known for meta-analytic and review work in strategic management. He co-authored the 2019 *Journal of Management* systematic review with Bergh, Orlandi, Heugens, and Boyd, which provides the foundational organizing framework for information asymmetry in management research.
