# Robert Bartlett

**Law professor at UC Berkeley School of Law; lead author of Bartlett et al. (2022), the JFE empirical study on racial discrimination in FinTech and non-FinTech mortgage lending.**

## Overview

Robert Bartlett is a professor at the University of California, Berkeley School of Law (225 Bancroft Way, Berkeley CA 94720). He is the first author of Bartlett, Morse, Stanton & Wallace (2022), published in the Journal of Financial Economics, which provides the most rigorous peer-reviewed empirical evidence to date that FinTech algorithmic lending reduces but does not eliminate racial discrimination in consumer mortgage pricing. [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]

His work bridges law and empirical finance, examining how regulatory frameworks interact with algorithmic pricing in mortgage markets.

## Key facts

- First author of Bartlett, Morse, Stanton & Wallace (2022), JFE 143(1): 30–56. [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]
