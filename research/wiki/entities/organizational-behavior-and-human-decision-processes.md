# Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes

**Peer-reviewed Elsevier journal covering judgment, decision-making, and organizational behavior; published Logg, Minson & Moore (2019) on algorithm appreciation.**

## Overview

Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes (OBHDP) is a leading peer-reviewed journal published by Elsevier covering judgment, decision-making, negotiation, and organizational behavior research. It published the algorithm appreciation paper (Logg, Minson & Moore 2019, volume 151, pp. 90–103). [[2026-05-07-logg-minson-moore-2019-algorithm-appreciation]]
