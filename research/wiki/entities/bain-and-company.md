# Bain & Company

**Global management consulting firm headquartered in Boston, MA; one of the three leading strategy consulting firms alongside McKinsey and BCG, with a prominent M&A advisory practice and an annual M&A Report.**

## Overview

Bain & Company publishes an annual M&A Report synthesising deal market trends and practitioner survey data. The 2024 M&A Report included a chapter titled "Generative AI in M&A: Where Hope Meets Hype," authored by Ben Siegal and Brooke Houston, which draws on the Bain M&A Practitioners' 2024 Outlook Survey of approximately 306 M&A practitioners. [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]]

The survey found that generative AI adoption in M&A stood at 16% in early 2024, with 80% of practitioners expecting adoption within three years — figures that have since been widely cited in practitioner commentary (including in Bremen 2024, which attributed them to a "BCG survey" rather than Bain). [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]]

## Key facts

- The Bain M&A Practitioners' 2024 Outlook Survey covered approximately 306 M&A practitioners (N=50 current GenAI users; N=256 non-users). [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]]
- The 2024 M&A Report is available at: https://www.bain.com/insights/generative-ai-m-and-a-report-2024/
- Bain's annual M&A Report covers overview, hot topics (including GenAI and regulation), and industry-specific views across 14 sectors. [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]]
