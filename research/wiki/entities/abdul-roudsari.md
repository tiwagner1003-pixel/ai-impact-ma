# Abdul Roudsari

**Health informatics researcher with dual affiliation at City University London and University of Victoria (British Columbia); co-author of the 2012 JAMIA automation bias systematic review.**

## Overview

Abdul Roudsari holds a dual affiliation at the Centre for Health Informatics, City University, London and the School of Health Information Science, University of Victoria, Victoria, British Columbia, Canada. He is a co-author of Goddard, Roudsari & Wyatt (2012), the foundational systematic review of automation bias across 74 empirical studies. [[2026-05-07-goddard-roudsari-wyatt-2012-automation-bias-systematic-review]]

## Key facts
