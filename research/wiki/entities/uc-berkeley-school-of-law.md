# UC Berkeley School of Law

**Law school at the University of California, Berkeley; institutional affiliation of Robert Bartlett on the Bartlett et al. (2022) FinTech lending discrimination paper.**

## Overview

UC Berkeley School of Law is one of the leading law schools in the United States, located at the University of California, Berkeley. Robert Bartlett, first author of the 2022 JFE study on consumer-lending discrimination in the FinTech era, is affiliated here.
