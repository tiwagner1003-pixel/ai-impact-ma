# Southeast University

**Public research university in Nanjing, China; primary institutional home of three of the four authors of Zhang et al. (2025).**

## Overview

Southeast University (SEU), located in Nanjing, Jiangsu Province (211189 PR China), is one of China's major research universities under the direct supervision of the Ministry of Education and a member of the "Double First Class" initiative. Its School of Economics and Management, together with the Institute of Digital Economy and Technological Innovation, produced the core author team of Zhang et al. (2025).
