# Ghent University

**Major Belgian public research university in Ghent; institutional affiliation of Floris Mertens, PhD Candidate and co-author of the 2025 AI risk allocation in M&A working paper.**

## Overview

Ghent University (Universiteit Gent) is one of the leading public research universities in Belgium, with strong faculties in law, social sciences, and natural sciences. It is the institutional home of Floris Mertens, a PhD candidate whose research on AI law contributed to the 2025 SSRN working paper on risk allocation in AI-guided M&A transactions.
