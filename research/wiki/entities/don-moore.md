# Don A. Moore

**Behavioral economist at the Haas School of Business, UC Berkeley, specializing in overconfidence, judgment, and decision-making; co-author of the algorithm appreciation paper.**

## Overview

Don A. Moore is a professor at the Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley. He is one of the leading researchers on overconfidence in judgment and decision-making. He is co-author, with Logg and Minson, of the algorithm appreciation study (2019) demonstrating that lay people systematically prefer algorithmic advice over human advice when the quality of advice is held equal. [[2026-05-07-logg-minson-moore-2019-algorithm-appreciation]]

His broader research program includes the "overconfidence" trilogy, Moore & Healy (2008) on the trouble with overconfidence, and Moore, Tenney & Haran (2015) on overconfidence in context.
