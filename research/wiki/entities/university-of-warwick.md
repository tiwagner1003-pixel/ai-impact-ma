# University of Warwick

**Public research university in Coventry, UK; institutional affiliation of Jeremy Wyatt (Institute of Digital Healthcare) on the 2012 automation bias systematic review.**

## Overview

The University of Warwick is a public research university in Coventry, UK. Jeremy C. Wyatt, senior co-author of the 2012 JAMIA automation bias systematic review, was affiliated with the Institute of Digital Healthcare at Warwick.
