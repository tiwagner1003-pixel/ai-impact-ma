# Brian K. Boyd

**Management professor at City University of Hong Kong and co-author of the Bergh et al. (2019) systematic review of information asymmetry in management research.**

## Overview

Brian K. Boyd is a management researcher at City University of Hong Kong whose work spans strategic management and corporate governance. He co-authored the 2019 *Journal of Management* review with Bergh, Ketchen, Orlandi, and Heugens. [[2026-05-07-bergh-et-al-2019-information-asymmetry-management-research]]
