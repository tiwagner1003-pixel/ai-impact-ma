# Floris Mertens

**PhD Candidate at Ghent University, Belgium; co-author of the 2025 SSRN working paper on AI risk allocation in M&A transactions.**

## Overview

Floris Mertens is a doctoral researcher at Ghent University, Belgium, specializing in AI law and corporate governance. He is co-author, together with Maarten Herbosch, of the 2025 SSRN working paper "The Future of Mergers & Acquisitions? Risk Allocation in AI-Guided Transactions," which provides a five-jurisdiction comparative legal analysis of liability exposure arising from AI tool use in M&A due diligence. [[2026-05-04-herbosch-mertens-2025-risk-allocation-ai-ma]]

The paper was supported by Fulbright and the Belgian American Educational Foundation; Mertens acknowledges Columbia Law School for discussions on the topic, indicating visiting or exchange engagement in New York. [[2026-05-04-herbosch-mertens-2025-risk-allocation-ai-ma]]
