# Washington State University

**Public research university in Pullman, Washington; home of the Carson College of Business, institutional affiliation of Adam Bozman and DJ Fairhurst (Bozman et al. 2026).**

## Overview

Washington State University (WSU) is a public research university headquartered in Pullman, Washington, USA. Its business school, the Carson College of Business, is the institutional home of [[adam-bozman]] and [[douglas-fairhurst]], co-authors of "Better Than a Coin Flip? Screening Mergers and Acquisitions With Artificial Intelligence Models" (SSRN 5421737, 2026). [[2026-05-04-bozman-et-al-2026-ai-deal-screening]]
