# Bachelorseminar M&A SS 2026

**Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt Bachelor seminar "Mergers & Acquisitions: Prozess und organisatorische Integration," taught by Prof. Dr. Lars Schweizer in summer semester 2026.**

## Overview

The seminar covers M&A from strategy through post-merger integration, organized around an integrative framework with eight paper topics. Groups of 3–4 students each write a seminar paper and deliver a presentation. The user is working on Thema 7: "Einfluss von AI auf M&A." [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]

The seminar is structured in three analytical layers: (I) M&A types and strategies, (II) the sequential deal process (due diligence → takeover tactics → synergy calculation → post-merger integration), and (III) M&A success measurement. Thema 6 (ESG) and Thema 7 (AI) are positioned as cross-cutting influence topics spanning layer II. [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]

## Academic writing requirements

The chair distributed a binding academic-writing guide (author: Marco Hochhaus) covering the following [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]:

- **Citation style**: Harvard in-text `(Author Jahr, Seite)`; indirect citations prefixed with "vgl."; plagiarism = automatic "mangelhaft."
- **Formatting**: 25–30 Textseiten incl. figures; Arial or Times New Roman 12 pt / 10 pt footnotes; 1.5-line body spacing / single footnote spacing; block justification; margins left 4 cm / right 2 cm / top 2.5 cm / bottom 3 cm.
- **Structure**: Einleitung → Grundlagenteil → Hauptteil → Schlussteil → Literaturverzeichnis; recommended writing order starts with Grundlagenteil.
- **AI use**: AI tools are not scientific sources; all AI use must be documented in a separate KI-Verzeichnis (table: tool + version, mode of use, affected sections); AI-generated citations must be independently verified.
- **Grading dimensions**: treatment of Fragestellung, results consistency, definitional precision, intellectual independence, language/style, structure, formal compliance.
