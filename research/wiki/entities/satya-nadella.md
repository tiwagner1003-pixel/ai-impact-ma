# Satya Nadella

**CEO of Microsoft, cited in Singh (2023) as having stated at a World Economic Forum panel that AI complements rather than replaces human capabilities.**

## Overview

Satya Nadella is the Chief Executive Officer of Microsoft. Singh (2023) references a World Economic Forum panel discussion at which Nadella (alongside Ginni Rometty) highlighted that AI complements rather than replaces human capabilities — a position used by Singh to frame AI as a decision-support aid in M&A, not a substitute for human judgment.
