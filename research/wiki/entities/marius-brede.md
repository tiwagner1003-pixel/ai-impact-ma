# Marius Brede

**Management researcher at Friedrich Schiller University Jena (Germany); lead author of the 2025 organizational cultural distance and M&A outcomes study using Culture-BERT on Glassdoor data.**

## Overview

Marius Brede is affiliated with the Friedrich Schiller University Jena (all four authors share superscript 1 in the paper, indicating the same institutional affiliation). He is the first author of Brede et al. (2025), published in the *Review of Managerial Science* (Vol. 19, pp. 2279–2326). [[2026-05-04-brede-et-al-2025-cultural-distance-ma-glassdoor]]

The paper applies the Culture-BERT transformer model to approximately 400,000 Glassdoor employee reviews to construct a novel measure of organizational cultural distance, linking it empirically to M&A capital market reactions, acquisition premiums, and post-deal innovativeness across 243 deals from 2008 to 2021. [[2026-05-04-brede-et-al-2025-cultural-distance-ma-glassdoor]]
