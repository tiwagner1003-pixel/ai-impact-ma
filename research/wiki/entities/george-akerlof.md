# George A. Akerlof

**Economist and author of the foundational "Market for Lemons" article on information asymmetry.**

## Overview

Akerlof's 1970 article formalizes how quality uncertainty can create adverse selection and market breakdown. In this wiki, he anchors the theoretical basis for using information asymmetry in the M&A Grundlagenteil. [[2026-05-05-akerlof-1970-market-for-lemons]]

## Key facts

- Author of "The Market for 'Lemons': Quality Uncertainty and the Market Mechanism." [[2026-05-05-akerlof-1970-market-for-lemons]]
