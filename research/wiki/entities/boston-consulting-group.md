# Boston Consulting Group

**Global management consulting firm headquartered in Boston, MA; four of the five authors of the MASS paper (Degen, Kengelbach, Kim, Wang) are BCG employees.**

## Overview

Boston Consulting Group (BCG) is one of the three leading global strategy consulting firms (alongside McKinsey and Bain). BCG has a substantial M&A advisory practice and a dedicated research and analysis function that publishes practitioner-oriented research on deal markets. [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]

The authorship composition of Degen et al. (2024) — four BCG practitioners co-authoring with a single academic (Soevers, Paderborn University) — reflects a practitioner-academic collaboration model. The BCG authors declare a competing interest: they receive salary from a firm that has commercial interest in M&A activity forecasting tools, which should be noted when assessing the paper's claims. [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]

## Key facts

- BCG Munich office affiliation for Degen, Kengelbach, Kim, Wang (Ludwigstrasse 21, 80539 Munich). [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]
- BCG has a global network of M&A experts who served as the human benchmark panel for validating MASS scores. [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]
- Bremen (2024) attributes figures of 16% current generative AI adoption in M&A and ~80% expected within three years to a "BCG survey." [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]

> ⚠️ Conflict: [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]] attributes the 16%/80% figures to a "BCG survey," but [[2026-05-04-bain-2024-genai-ma-hope-meets-hype]] presents the same statistics as data from the Bain M&A Practitioners' 2024 Outlook Survey (N≈306). The primary source of these figures is Bain & Company, not BCG. Whether BCG published a separate survey with coincidentally identical results is unknown. For citation purposes, use [[bain-and-company]] as the primary source. Unresolved.
