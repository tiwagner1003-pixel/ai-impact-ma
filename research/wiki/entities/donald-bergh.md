# Donald D. Bergh

**Management professor at the University of Denver (Daniels College of Business) and lead author of the 2019 Journal of Management systematic review on information asymmetry.**

## Overview

Donald D. Bergh is a strategy and management scholar whose work focuses on information asymmetry, divestiture decisions, and strategic management theory. His 2019 review paper in the *Journal of Management* (co-authored with Ketchen, Orlandi, Heugens, and Boyd) is the first systematic review of information asymmetry as a management concept, covering 223 articles across six leading journals. [[2026-05-07-bergh-et-al-2019-information-asymmetry-management-research]]
