# Haas School of Business

**Business school at the University of California, Berkeley; institutional affiliation of Don Moore (algorithm appreciation paper) and of Adair Morse, Richard Stanton, and Nancy Wallace (FinTech discrimination paper).**

## Overview

The Haas School of Business is the business school of the University of California, Berkeley. It is the institutional affiliation of Don A. Moore (co-author of Logg, Minson & Moore 2019 on algorithm appreciation) [[2026-05-07-logg-minson-moore-2019-algorithm-appreciation]] and of Adair Morse, Richard Stanton, and Nancy Wallace (co-authors of Bartlett et al. 2022 on FinTech lending discrimination).
