# Kristin Johnson

**McGlinchey Stafford Professor of Law and Gordon Gamm Faculty Fellow at Tulane University Law School; legal scholar specialising in financial regulation, derivatives, and the governance of AI in financial services.**

## Overview

Kristin Johnson is lead author of the 2019 Fordham Law Review essay on AI, machine learning, and bias in finance. She was, at the time of writing, McGlinchey Stafford Professor of Law at Tulane University Law School and Associate Dean of Faculty Research. Her scholarship addresses the intersection of financial regulation, antidiscrimination law, and algorithmic decision-making in consumer credit markets. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]

## Key facts

- McGlinchey Stafford Professor of Law, Tulane University Law School (at time of publication, 2019). [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
