# Douglas (DJ) Fairhurst

**Finance professor at the Carson College of Business, Washington State University; co-author of the 2026 AI deal-screening paper and prior work on ChatGPT's financial knowledge.**

## Overview

Douglas (DJ) Fairhurst is a finance professor at the [[washington-state-university|Carson College of Business, Washington State University]]. He is a co-author (with [[adam-bozman]] and [[daniel-greene]]) of "Better Than a Coin Flip? Screening Mergers and Acquisitions With Artificial Intelligence Models" (SSRN 5421737, March 2026). [[2026-05-04-bozman-et-al-2026-ai-deal-screening]]

Fairhurst has prior collaborative work with Greene on the financial knowledge of ChatGPT. The paper "How Much Does ChatGPT Know about Finance?" (Fairhurst and Greene, 2025, *Financial Analysts Journal*, 81, 12–32) is cited in the AI deal-screening paper as the methodological basis for the LLM prompting parameter choices (low randomness, moderate frequency and presence penalty). [[2026-05-04-bozman-et-al-2026-ai-deal-screening]]
