# The Atticus Project

**The Atticus Project is the legal organization associated with the creation of [[maud-dataset]], the expert-annotated merger-agreement understanding benchmark.**

## Relevance

The organization matters for the seminar paper as the source context for both MAUD and CUAD. Its role strengthens dataset credibility because annotations were produced through legal-domain processes rather than generic crowdsourcing.
