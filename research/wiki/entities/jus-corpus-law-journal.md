# Jus Corpus Law Journal

**An Indian open-access law journal (ISSN 2582-7820) that published Singh (2023) on AI's transformative potential in M&A transactions.**

## Overview

Jus Corpus Law Journal is an open-access publication distributed under a Creative Commons Attribution-Non-Commercial-Share Alike 4.0 International (CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0) licence. The journal's Editor-in-Chief is Prof. (Dr.) Rhishikesh Dave; publisher is Ayush Pandey. [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]

Singh (2023) — the prescribed Thema 7 reading — was published in Vol. 3, Issue 4 (June–August 2023) of this journal, pp. 249–260. The Bluebook citation style renders the year as "2022" (the volume year convention), which is why the seminar syllabus lists the paper as "Singh (2022)."
