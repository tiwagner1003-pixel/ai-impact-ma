# John M. Bremen

**Senior Managing Director and Chief Innovation and Acceleration Officer at Willis Towers Watson (WTW); author of the practitioner article "AI accelerates M&A into the future" (2024).**

## Overview

John M. Bremen is a senior executive at WTW (Willis Towers Watson), serving as Senior Managing Director and Chief Innovation and Acceleration Officer. He writes practitioner-facing thought leadership on AI, work transformation, and M&A strategy, publishing both on the WTW website and in Forbes. [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]

The article "AI accelerates M&A into the future" (published Forbes May 31, 2024; WTW June 26, 2024) is one of the four prescribed readings for Thema 7 ("Der Einfluss von KI auf M&A") of the Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt Bachelor seminar SS 2026. [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]

## Key facts

- Title: Senior Managing Director and Chief Innovation and Acceleration Officer, WTW. [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]
- The WTW AI M&A article was co-developed with WTW colleague Steven Rosenberg and informed by a Fortune 500 M&A roundtable facilitated by WTW. [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]
- Article originally published in Forbes (May 31, 2024); republished by WTW (June 26, 2024). [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]
