# Alejandro Barredo Arrieta

**AI researcher affiliated with TECNALIA (Derio, Spain); lead author of the landmark 2020 XAI survey paper in *Information Fusion* (6,000+ citations).**

## Overview

Alejandro Barredo Arrieta is a researcher at TECNALIA, a large Spanish applied research and technology centre based in the Basque Country (Derio, Bizkaia). He is the first author and primary contributor to the 2020 survey "Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI): Concepts, taxonomies, opportunities and challenges toward responsible AI," published in *Information Fusion* (Vol. 58, pp. 82–115). This paper is among the most cited works in the XAI field. [[2026-05-04-arrieta-et-al-2020-xai-concepts-taxonomies]]

Per the CRediT authorship statement, Arrieta's contributions were: Conceptualization, Investigation, Writing — original draft, Writing — review & editing, Supervision. He is co-corresponding author alongside Javier Del Ser. [[2026-05-04-arrieta-et-al-2020-xai-concepts-taxonomies]]
