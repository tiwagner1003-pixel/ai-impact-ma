# Gábor Stikkel

**Data scientist at the Data Science Lab of Clifford Chance (UK); co-author of the first NLP/LLM study on M&A due diligence (NAACL 2024), providing the legal industry perspective.**

## Overview

Gábor Stikkel is affiliated with the Data Science Lab at [[clifford-chance]] in the UK (email: gabor.stikkel@cliffordchance.com). He is the second author of Jang & Stikkel (2024), "Leveraging Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models for Assisting Due Diligence in the Legal Domain," published in NAACL 2024 Industry Track (pp. 155–164). [[2026-05-04-jang-stikkel-2024-nlp-llms-due-diligence]]

Stikkel's role represents the practitioner side of the Oxford–Clifford Chance collaboration: embedding the academic NLP research in the operational context of a major international law firm conducting M&A due diligence. The industry track venue of the paper reflects this practical grounding. [[2026-05-04-jang-stikkel-2024-nlp-llms-due-diligence]]
