# Gower Publishing

**UK academic and professional publisher (now an imprint of Routledge/Taylor & Francis); original publisher of Howson's due diligence textbook in 2003.**

## Overview

Gower Publishing was a UK-based publisher specialising in management, business, and professional practice books. It published Peter Howson's *Due Diligence: The Critical Stage in Mergers and Acquisitions* in 2003. Gower was subsequently absorbed into the Routledge/Taylor & Francis Group (an Informa business), which reissued the book in 2017 under the Routledge imprint.
