# Kai Li

**Finance scholar and lead author of the RFS paper on measuring corporate culture with machine learning.**

## Overview

Li et al. (2021) use word embeddings and earnings-call transcripts to measure corporate culture and relate it to firm outcomes, including deal making. [[2026-05-05-li-et-al-2021-corporate-culture-ml]]
