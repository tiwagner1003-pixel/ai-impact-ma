# Economic Analysis and Policy

**Peer-reviewed international economics journal published by Elsevier on behalf of the Economic Society of Australia; venue for Zhang et al. (2025) on digital transformation and M&A.**

## Overview

*Economic Analysis and Policy* (EAP) is a quarterly peer-reviewed journal published by Elsevier (ISSN 0313-5926) for the Economic Society of Australia (Queensland Branch). It covers applied economics, economic policy, and interdisciplinary research at the intersection of economics and policy-relevant fields. Zhang et al. (2025) was published in Volume 86 (June 2025), pp. 764–778, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eap.2025.03.049. [[2026-05-04-zhang-et-al-2025-digital-transformation-ma-information-asymmetry]]
