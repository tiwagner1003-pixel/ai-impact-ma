# Fordham Law Review

**Peer-reviewed law journal published by Fordham University School of Law in New York; publication venue for Johnson, Pasquale & Chapman (2019) on AI, machine learning, and bias in finance.**

## Overview

The Fordham Law Review is one of the leading American law reviews, published at Fordham University School of Law in New York City. Vol. 88, No. 2 (2019) contained the symposium "Rise of the Machines: Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and the Reprogramming of Law," hosted by the Fordham Law Review and the Neuroscience and Law Center on February 15, 2019. Johnson, Pasquale & Chapman's essay appeared in this symposium issue (pp. 499–530). [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]

## Key facts

- Published Johnson, Pasquale & Chapman (2019) on AI and bias in finance (Vol. 88, No. 2, pp. 499–530). [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
- Hosted the "Rise of the Machines" symposium on AI, robotics, and law (February 15, 2019). [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
