# Kavya Sanjay Singh

**Legal scholar at National Law University, Delhi, and author of the 2023 survey paper on AI's transformative potential in M&A transactions.**

## Overview

Kavya Sanjay Singh is affiliated with the National Law University, Delhi, India. She authored "The Transformative Potential of Artificial Intelligence and its Impact on M&A Transactions," published in the Jus Corpus Law Journal (Vol. 3, Issue 4, 2023, pp. 249–260). [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]

The paper is a legal-practitioner survey of AI applications across the M&A lifecycle. It is one of the four prescribed readings for Thema 7 ("Der Einfluss von KI auf M&A") in the SS 2026 Bachelor seminar at Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt. [[2026-05-04-singh-2023-ai-transformative-potential-ma]]
