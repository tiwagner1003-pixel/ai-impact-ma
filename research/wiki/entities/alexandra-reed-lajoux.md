# Alexandra Reed Lajoux

**M&A practitioner, scholar, and author; principal author of *The Art of M&A*, the standard comprehensive Q&A-format practitioner guide to the full M&A lifecycle across six editions (1989–2024).**

## Overview

Alexandra Reed Lajoux is the lead author of *The Art of M&A: A Merger, Acquisition, and Buyout Guide*, now in its sixth and final edition (McGraw-Hill Education, 2024), co-produced with [[capital-expert-services]]. The book has been revised across six editions since 1989, making it one of the most durable practitioner references in M&A. [[2026-05-07-lajoux-2024-art-of-ma-sixth-edition-ch1-ch6]]

She is affiliated with Capital Expert Services, LLC (co-owned with John Hotta), a think tank and consultancy dedicated to helping law firms and professional services firms find qualified expert witnesses in areas outside the ordinary. Her work distinguishes itself from specialist DD references (e.g., [[peter-howson]]) by covering the full M&A lifecycle — strategy, valuation, financing, structuring, due diligence, negotiation, closing, and post-merger integration — in a single comprehensive Q&A-format volume. [[2026-05-07-lajoux-2024-art-of-ma-sixth-edition-ch1-ch6]]

Her father Stanley Foster Reed founded *Mergers & Acquisitions: The Journal of Corporate Venture* in 1964 and collaborated with Lajoux on the first edition of the book in 1988 alongside the law firm Lane & Edson. He passed away at age 90 in 2007 after the publication of the fourth edition. The sixth edition — the final volume in the McGraw-Hill series — is explicitly described as a fitting conclusion to a series that "changed the way we think about M&A." [[2026-05-07-lajoux-2024-art-of-ma-sixth-edition-ch1-ch6]]

The 2024 edition reflects M&A developments from 2019 to early 2024, including pandemic effects on dealmaking, the rise of remote work, growing AI application in M&A (including an explicit reference to ChatGPT in the preface and a Chapter 3 checklist for AI-embedded asset valuation), post-pandemic M&A rebound signals, and updated legal and regulatory developments. [[2026-05-07-lajoux-2024-art-of-ma-sixth-edition-ch1-ch6]]

## Key facts

- Lead author of *The Art of M&A*, with editions in 1989, 1995, 1999, 2007, 2019, and 2024. [[2026-05-07-lajoux-2024-art-of-ma-sixth-edition-ch1-ch6]]
- Sixth edition (2024) co-produced with [[capital-expert-services]], LLC, published by [[mcgraw-hill]]; described as the final volume in the McGraw-Hill series. [[2026-05-07-lajoux-2024-art-of-ma-sixth-edition-ch1-ch6]]
- The 2024 edition carries a foreword by Jim Jeffries ([[jim-jeffries]]), Founder of the [[ma-leadership-council]] and Board of M&A Standards (BMAS). [[2026-05-07-lajoux-2024-art-of-ma-sixth-edition-ch1-ch6]]
- Lajoux holds emerita status on the Board of M&A Standards (BMAS). [[2026-05-07-lajoux-2024-art-of-ma-sixth-edition-ch1-ch6]]
- Chapter 6 of the 2024 edition ("The Due Diligence Inquiry") benefits from expertise of Charles M. Elson, Corporate Director and Director of the John L. Weinberg Center for Corporate Governance at the University of Delaware; and is co-authored in prior editions with Lajoux as *The Art of M&A Due Diligence* (McGraw-Hill, 2000, 2010).
