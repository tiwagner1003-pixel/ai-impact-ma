# Myeongjun Erik Jang

**NLP researcher at the Department of Computer Science, University of Oxford; first author of the first study applying PLMs and LLMs to M&A due diligence (NAACL 2024).**

## Overview

Myeongjun Erik Jang is affiliated with the Department of Computer Science at the University of Oxford (email: myeongjun.jang@cs.ox.ac.uk). He is the first author of Jang & Stikkel (2024), "Leveraging Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models for Assisting Due Diligence in the Legal Domain," published in the Proceedings of NAACL 2024 Industry Track (pp. 155–164). [[2026-05-04-jang-stikkel-2024-nlp-llms-due-diligence]]

The paper represents an industry-academia collaboration with [[clifford-chance]]'s Data Science Lab, grounding the NLP research in real legal practitioner requirements. [[2026-05-04-jang-stikkel-2024-nlp-llms-due-diligence]]
