# Jennifer M. Logg

**Behavioral scientist at Harvard Kennedy School studying how people perceive and respond to algorithmic versus human advice; lead author of the "algorithm appreciation" paper.**

## Overview

Jennifer M. Logg is a researcher at the Harvard Kennedy School, Harvard University. Her work sits at the intersection of judgment and decision-making, organizational behavior, and human-algorithm interaction. She is best known for the "algorithm appreciation" study (Logg, Minson & Moore 2019), which demonstrated — across six pre-registered experiments — that lay people systematically give more weight to advice labeled as algorithmic than to identical advice from a human advisor. [[2026-05-07-logg-minson-moore-2019-algorithm-appreciation]]

Her prior collaborative work includes overconfidence research with Moore and Haran (2018), egocentric discounting of advice, and the wisdom-of-dyads literature (Liberman, Minson, Bryan & Ross 2012).
