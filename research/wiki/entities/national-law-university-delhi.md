# National Law University, Delhi

**A public law university in Delhi, India, and the institutional affiliation of Kavya Sanjay Singh, author of the 2023 AI-in-M&A survey paper.**

## Overview

National Law University, Delhi (NLU Delhi) is one of India's premier public law schools. It is the affiliation listed for Kavya Sanjay Singh in her 2023 survey article on AI's transformative potential in M&A transactions, published in the Jus Corpus Law Journal.
