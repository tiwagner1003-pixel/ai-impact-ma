# KPMG

**Global professional services network (audit, tax, advisory); cited in Howson (2003) for a 1999 study on the keys to acquisition success.**

## Overview

KPMG is one of the "Big Four" global professional services firms, providing audit, tax, and advisory services. In the context of this wiki, KPMG is relevant as the source of a 1999 study on acquisition success factors cited extensively in Chapter 1 of Howson (2003). That study identified six keys to deal success — three "hard keys" (synergy evaluation, integration project planning, due diligence) and three "soft keys" (selecting the management team, resolving cultural issues, communications). [[2026-05-07-howson-2003-due-diligence-critical-stage]]

The KPMG 1999 study has not been separately ingested into this wiki. Its findings are available only as cited by Howson.

## Key facts

- Authored a 1999 study on acquisition success factors (title and full citation not available from the preview). [[2026-05-07-howson-2003-due-diligence-critical-stage]]
- The study found an 83% correlation between integration implementation success and overall acquisition success. [[2026-05-07-howson-2003-due-diligence-critical-stage]]
- Pre-deal synergy evaluation identified as the top hard key (+28% above average deal success rate). [[2026-05-07-howson-2003-due-diligence-critical-stage]]
- "Resolving cultural issues" identified as the top soft key to acquisition success. [[2026-05-07-howson-2003-due-diligence-critical-stage]]
