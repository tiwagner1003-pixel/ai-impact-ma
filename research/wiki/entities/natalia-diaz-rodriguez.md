# Natalia Díaz-Rodríguez

**AI researcher with dual affiliation at ENSTA, Institut Polytechnique Paris and INRIA Flowers Team; second author of the 2020 XAI survey in *Information Fusion*.**

## Overview

Natalia Díaz-Rodríguez is an AI researcher affiliated with ENSTA, Institut Polytechnique Paris, and the INRIA Flowers Team (Palaiseau, France) at the time of the 2020 XAI survey. She contributed to the conceptualization, investigation, writing of the original draft, and review and editing of "Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI): Concepts, taxonomies, opportunities and challenges toward responsible AI."
