# Richard Stanton

**Finance professor at UC Berkeley Haas School of Business; corresponding author of Bartlett et al. (2022) on FinTech lending discrimination.**

## Overview

Richard Stanton is a professor at the Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley. He is the corresponding author of Bartlett, Morse, Stanton & Wallace (2022), published in the Journal of Financial Economics, which provides large-sample empirical evidence of racial discrimination in both FinTech and traditional mortgage lending. [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]
