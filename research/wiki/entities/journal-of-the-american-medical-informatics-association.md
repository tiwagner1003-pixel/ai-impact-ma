# Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association

**Peer-reviewed journal of biomedical and health informatics; published Goddard, Roudsari & Wyatt (2012) on the systematic review of automation bias.**

## Overview

JAMIA is the flagship peer-reviewed journal of the American Medical Informatics Association, covering biomedical and health informatics research. It published the foundational systematic review on automation bias by Goddard, Roudsari & Wyatt (2012), which synthesized 74 studies across healthcare, aviation, and HCI and produced the meta-analytic estimate that erroneous DSS advice increases incorrect decisions by 26% (RR=1.26).
