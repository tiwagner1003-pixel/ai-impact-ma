# Nancy Wallace

**Finance professor at UC Berkeley Haas School of Business specializing in real estate finance; co-author of Bartlett et al. (2022) on FinTech lending discrimination.**

## Overview

Nancy Wallace is a professor at the Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley. She is co-author of Bartlett, Morse, Stanton & Wallace (2022), published in the Journal of Financial Economics. Her expertise in real estate finance and mortgage markets contributed to the paper's identification strategy and data construction.
