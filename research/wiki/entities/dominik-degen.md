# Dominik Degen

**Corresponding author of the 2024 MASS paper; practitioner-researcher at Boston Consulting Group (BCG), Munich.**

## Overview

Dominik Degen is the corresponding author and lead contributor on "Large Language Models and M&A: Can ChatGPT help forecast M&A activity?" (2024), co-authored with Kengelbach, Kim, Sievers, and Wang. His roles on the paper include Conceptualization, Methodology, Formal Analysis, Software, Visualization, Writing, and Project Administration. [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]

He is affiliated with Boston Consulting Group (Ludwigstrasse 21, 80539 Munich) and is reachable at degen.dominik@bcg.com. [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]
