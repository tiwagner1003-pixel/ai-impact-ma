# CSMAR Database

**China Stock Market & Accounting Research (CSMAR) database: a comprehensive data platform covering Chinese A-share listed companies' financial, accounting, and transaction data, including a dedicated Digital Transformation Research Database.**

## Overview

CSMAR is the primary data source for Chinese listed-company empirical research in finance and accounting. It provides standardised financial statements, M&A transaction records, board composition data, ownership structure, and a range of firm-level indicators for all A-share listed companies. Zhang et al. (2025) use CSMAR as the source of M&A event data and control variables (firm size, leverage, ROA, board composition, institutional ownership, etc.) for their 2008–2022 panel dataset of 22,274 firm-year observations. [[2026-05-04-zhang-et-al-2025-digital-transformation-ma-information-asymmetry]]

CSMAR also releases the "China Listed Companies Digital Transformation Research Database," which provides a firm-level digital transformation index (DT5 in Zhang et al.'s robustness tests) based on its own text-analysis methodology. This index is used as one of five alternative DT measures in Zhang et al. (2025), with consistent results. [[2026-05-04-zhang-et-al-2025-digital-transformation-ma-information-asymmetry]]

## Key facts

- Primary use in Zhang et al. (2025): source of M&A transaction data and firm-level control variables for Chinese A-share companies 2008–2022. [[2026-05-04-zhang-et-al-2025-digital-transformation-ma-information-asymmetry]]
- Provides a dedicated Digital Transformation index (DT5) for listed companies. [[2026-05-04-zhang-et-al-2025-digital-transformation-ma-information-asymmetry]]
- Complementary database: CNRDS (Chinese Research Data Services), which provides the primary digital transformation keyword data used by Zhang et al. [[2026-05-04-zhang-et-al-2025-digital-transformation-ma-information-asymmetry]]
