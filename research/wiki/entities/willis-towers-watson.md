# Willis Towers Watson

**Global advisory, broking, and solutions company offering risk management, insurance, human capital, and M&A advisory services; publisher of the Bremen (2024) practitioner article on AI in M&A.**

## Overview

Willis Towers Watson (WTW) is a multinational professional services firm operating in risk, capital, and people advisory. In the M&A context, WTW provides transaction advisory services including human capital due diligence, benefits integration, and workforce transformation support. [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]

WTW hosted a Fortune 500 M&A roundtable that informed Bremen's 2024 article on AI in M&A. The article represents WTW's practitioner perspective on AI adoption trajectories and responsible governance frameworks for deal teams. WTW has a direct commercial interest in M&A advisory services, which should be weighed when assessing the article's normative claims. [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]
