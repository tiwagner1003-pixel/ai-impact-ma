# Kate Goddard

**Health informatics researcher at City University London; lead author of the foundational 2012 JAMIA systematic review of automation bias across 74 studies.**

## Overview

Kate Goddard is a researcher at the Centre for Health Informatics, City University, London. She led the systematic review of automation bias (Goddard, Roudsari & Wyatt, JAMIA 2012) that synthesized evidence from 74 studies across healthcare, aviation, and human-computer interaction. The review produced a meta-analytic risk ratio of 1.26 — the most widely cited quantitative estimate of how erroneous decision support increases decision errors. [[2026-05-07-goddard-roudsari-wyatt-2012-automation-bias-systematic-review]]

The review was carried out as part of ongoing PhD research funded by the Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council (EPSRC).
