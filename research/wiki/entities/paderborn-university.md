# Paderborn University

**German public research university in Paderborn; home institution of Soenke Sievers, the academic co-author of the MASS paper and a participant in the TRR 266 Accounting for Transparency collaborative research center.**

## Overview

Paderborn University (Universität Paderborn) is a mid-sized German university with a Faculty of Business Administration and Economics. Prof. Soenke Sievers (Department of Taxation, Accounting and Finance) is a co-author of Degen et al. (2024) and leads his contribution to the paper through funding from the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) under the TRR 266 Accounting for Transparency collaborative research center. [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]

Paderborn University is also home to the TRR 266 contact address (Prof. Dr. Caren Sureth-Sloane, Department of Taxation), which publishes the working paper series that includes the MASS paper as Working Paper No. 150. [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]
