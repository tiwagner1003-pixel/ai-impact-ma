# Yuehua Tang

**Finance professor at the University of Florida (Warrington College of Business); co-author of Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu (2025) on the LLM memorization problem in economic forecasting.**

## Overview

Yuehua Tang is a finance researcher at the University of Florida. He co-authored the earlier Lopez-Lira and Tang (2023) paper on LLMs and stock returns, which the memorization paper cites as a methodologically valid study because it restricted analysis to post-training-cutoff data. This earlier work serves as a positive example of how to avoid the memorization problem. [[2026-05-04-lopez-lira-et-al-2025-memorization-problem-llms]]
