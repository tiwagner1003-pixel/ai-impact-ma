# Jeremy C. Wyatt

**Clinical informatics and digital health researcher at the Institute of Digital Healthcare, University of Warwick; co-author of the 2012 JAMIA automation bias systematic review.**

## Overview

Jeremy C. Wyatt is a researcher at the Institute of Digital Healthcare, University of Warwick, UK. He is a senior co-author of Goddard, Roudsari & Wyatt (2012), the JAMIA systematic review establishing automation bias as a 26%-risk-ratio effect across clinical decision support systems. [[2026-05-07-goddard-roudsari-wyatt-2012-automation-bias-systematic-review]]

## Key facts

- Co-author of Goddard, Roudsari & Wyatt (2012), JAMIA 19(1): 121–127. [[2026-05-07-goddard-roudsari-wyatt-2012-automation-bias-systematic-review]]
