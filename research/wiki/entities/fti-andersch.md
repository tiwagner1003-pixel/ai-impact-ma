# FTI Andersch

**German restructuring and transaction advisory firm serving as the Praxispartner (industry guest) for the SS 2026 Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt Bachelor seminar on M&A.**

## Overview

FTI Andersch is the industry practice partner for the SS 2026 M&A Bachelor seminar at Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt. On seminar Day 1 (Monday, 8 June 2026), FTI Andersch representatives deliver a Gastvortrag (keynote guest lecture) and then serve as Diskussionspartner for all presentations given that day. [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-syllabus]]

FTI Andersch is the German arm of FTI Consulting, a global business advisory firm. The Andersch unit specializes in restructuring, insolvency advisory, and transaction services — areas that naturally intersect with M&A deal process, due diligence, and post-merger integration.
