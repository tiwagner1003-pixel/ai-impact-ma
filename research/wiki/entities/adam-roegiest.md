# Adam Roegiest

**Lead author of Roegiest, Hudek & McNulty (SIGIR 2018), the foundational paper and dataset release for M&A due diligence passage retrieval; affiliated with Kira Systems (now Zuva) at time of publication.**

## Overview

Adam Roegiest is the first author of "A Dataset and an Examination of Identifying Passages for Due Diligence" (SIGIR 2018), which introduced the KIRA dataset — the only publicly available benchmark for M&A legal due diligence passage retrieval. The paper and dataset are reproduced and extended by Dwivedi & Kamps (2025) and serve as the primary benchmark baseline for Jang & Stikkel (2024). [[2026-05-07-dwivedi-kamps-2025-icl-due-diligence]] [[2026-05-04-jang-stikkel-2024-nlp-llms-due-diligence]]

Roegiest is also co-author of "Questions about Contracts: Prompt Templates for Structured Answer Generation" (NLP-Legal Workshop 2023), which demonstrated that prompt engineering with GPT-3.5-Turbo outperformed traditional contract analysis methods in accuracy and consistency for legal question-answering tasks. [[2026-05-07-dwivedi-kamps-2025-icl-due-diligence]]
