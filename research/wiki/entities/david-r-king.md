# David R. King

**Lead author of the 2004 meta-analysis on post-acquisition performance.**

## Overview

King et al. (2004) synthesize post-acquisition performance research and find that acquirers do not, on average, improve performance after acquisitions. [[2026-05-05-king-et-al-2004-post-acquisition-performance-meta-analysis]]
