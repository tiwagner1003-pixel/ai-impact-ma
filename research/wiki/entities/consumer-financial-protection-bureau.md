# Consumer Financial Protection Bureau

**U.S. federal regulatory agency created by the Dodd-Frank Act (2010) to enforce consumer financial protection and antidiscrimination laws in financial services markets; cited by Johnson et al. (2019) as a key institution for establishing minimum ADM transparency and bias-mitigation standards in fintech.**

## Overview

The CFPB was established by the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act (2010) with authority over consumer financial services including lending, credit scoring, and debt collection. Johnson et al. (2019) argue the CFPB could serve an important role in establishing minimum standards for the integration of ADM technology in fintech, ensuring transparency, explainability, and compliance with consumer protection and antidiscrimination norms. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]

The authors note, however, that the CFPB's personnel priorities and reorganisation following 2017 raise concerns about rigorous enforcement — citing Wells Fargo scandals as evidence that even national banking regulators may not rigorously monitor compliance with consumer protections. The paper also discusses the CFPB's 2013 bulletin on disparate-impact liability for indirect auto lending as an example of the CFPB applying Equal Credit Opportunity Act antidiscrimination norms to algorithmic pricing. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]

## Key facts

- Created by Dodd-Frank Act (2010), 12 U.S.C. § 5491. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
- Has authority to establish minimum standards for ADM integration in consumer financial services, including transparency and explainability requirements. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
- Issued a 2013 bulletin applying ECOA disparate-impact liability to indirect auto-lending algorithmic pricing. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
- Personnel and priority changes from 2017 onward raised concerns about robust enforcement capacity. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
