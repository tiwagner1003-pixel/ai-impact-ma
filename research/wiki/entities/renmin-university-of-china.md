# Renmin University of China

**Major public research university in Beijing, China; primary institutional affiliation of the authors of "A Survey of Large Language Models" (Zhao et al. 2023), specifically the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence and School of Information.**

## Overview

Renmin University of China (RUC) is one of China's leading comprehensive universities. Its Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence is one of the primary centers of NLP and LLM research in China. Wayne Xin Zhao and the majority of co-authors of the Zhao et al. (2023) LLM survey paper are affiliated with RUC. [[2026-05-05-zhao-et-al-2023-survey-large-language-models]]

The survey's companion GitHub repository is maintained by RUC under the organization RUCAIBox at https://github.com/RUCAIBox/LLMSurvey.

## Key facts

- Primary institutional home of Zhao et al. (2023), the most widely cited LLM survey (~15,000+ citations). [[2026-05-05-zhao-et-al-2023-survey-large-language-models]]
- Relevant departments: Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence; School of Information. [[2026-05-05-zhao-et-al-2023-survey-large-language-models]]
- One co-author (Jian-Yun Nie) is with DIRO, Université de Montréal (not RUC). [[2026-05-05-zhao-et-al-2023-survey-large-language-models]]
