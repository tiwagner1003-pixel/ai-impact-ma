# Mingyin Zhu

**PhD student or researcher at the University of Florida (Warrington College of Business); corresponding third author of Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu (2025) on the LLM memorization problem.**

## Overview

Mingyin Zhu is listed as the contact/corresponding author (marked with asterisk in the paper) for Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu (2025). The paper documents systematic memorization of economic and financial data by LLMs and formalizes why this renders pre-cutoff LLM forecasting studies non-identified.
