# Wayne Xin Zhao

**NLP researcher at Renmin University of China; lead and corresponding author of "A Survey of Large Language Models" (arXiv:2303.18223), the most widely cited LLM survey paper (~15,000+ citations).**

## Overview

Wayne Xin Zhao is affiliated with the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence and the School of Information at Renmin University of China, Beijing. He is the lead author of the landmark LLM survey paper Zhao et al. (2023), which provides the canonical four-generation taxonomy of language models and the definitional framework for LLMs used in subsequent academic work. [[2026-05-05-zhao-et-al-2023-survey-large-language-models]]

The survey covers pre-training, adaptation tuning, utilization via prompting, and capability evaluation across a wide range of LLMs. The paper has been updated through multiple arXiv versions (v13 dated November 24, 2023) reflecting the rapid pace of LLM development.
