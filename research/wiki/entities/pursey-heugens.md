# Pursey P. M. A. R. Heugens

**Strategy and organization professor at Erasmus University Rotterdam and co-author of the Bergh et al. (2019) systematic review of information asymmetry in management research.**

## Overview

Pursey Heugens is a professor at Erasmus University Rotterdam known for institutional theory and organization research. He co-authored the 2019 *Journal of Management* review with Bergh, Ketchen, Orlandi, and Boyd. [[2026-05-07-bergh-et-al-2019-information-asymmetry-management-research]]
