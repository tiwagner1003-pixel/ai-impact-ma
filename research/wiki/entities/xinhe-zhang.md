# Xinhe Zhang

**Researcher at the School of Economics and Management, Southeast University, Nanjing; first author of Zhang et al. (2025) on digital transformation and M&A.**

## Overview

Xinhe Zhang is affiliated with the School of Economics and Management at Southeast University, Nanjing, China (211189 PR China).
