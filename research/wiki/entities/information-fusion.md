# Information Fusion (journal)

**Peer-reviewed Elsevier journal (ISSN 1566-2535) covering data fusion, information integration, and related ML/AI research; publication venue for the Arrieta et al. (2020) XAI survey.**

## Overview

*Information Fusion* is an international peer-reviewed journal published by Elsevier, specializing in research on the combination of information from multiple sources — including sensor fusion, data fusion, knowledge fusion, and the integration of ML/AI models. The journal's scope covers fusion theory, methods, and applications across engineering, computer science, and applied sciences. [[2026-05-04-arrieta-et-al-2020-xai-concepts-taxonomies]]

The Arrieta et al. (2020) XAI survey was published in Volume 58 (2020), pp. 82–115, making it one of the journal's most highly cited papers. Accepted 25 December 2019; available online 26 December 2019. [[2026-05-04-arrieta-et-al-2020-xai-concepts-taxonomies]]
