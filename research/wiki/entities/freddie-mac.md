# Freddie Mac

**U.S. government-sponsored enterprise (GSE) that securitizes residential mortgages alongside Fannie Mae; jointly sets the LLPA pricing grid used as the identification strategy in Bartlett et al. (2022).**

## Overview

Freddie Mac (Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation) is the second of the two U.S. government-sponsored enterprises. Like Fannie Mae, it guarantees residential mortgages and sets credit-risk pricing via the LLPA grid (using Credit Fees in Price, the Freddie Mac equivalent). The Bartlett et al. (2022) study covers GSE-securitized loans from both Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]

## Key facts

- LLPA equivalent grid used by Freddie Mac is "Credit Fees in Price." [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]
- Jointly covers the GSE-securitized sample with [[fannie-mae]] in Bartlett et al. (2022). [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]
