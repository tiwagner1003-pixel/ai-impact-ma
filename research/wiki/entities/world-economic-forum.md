# World Economic Forum

**International organisation for public-private cooperation; cited in Singh (2023) as the venue of a panel where Ginni Rometty and Satya Nadella argued that AI complements rather than replaces human capabilities.**

## Overview

The World Economic Forum (WEF) is an international non-governmental organisation headquartered in Cologny, Switzerland, known for its annual meeting in Davos. Singh (2023) references a WEF panel discussion at which IBM's Ginni Rometty and Microsoft's Satya Nadella highlighted that AI is a complement to human capabilities — a claim used to frame AI's role in M&A as augmentative rather than substitutive.
