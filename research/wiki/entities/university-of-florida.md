# University of Florida

**Public research university in Gainesville, Florida; institutional affiliation of Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu (2025), authors of the LLM memorization problem paper.**

## Overview

The University of Florida's Warrington College of Business hosts the finance research group that produced Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu (2025). The paper is the primary challenge to the validity of LLM-based economic forecasting on pre-training-cutoff data. [[2026-05-04-lopez-lira-et-al-2025-memorization-problem-llms]]
