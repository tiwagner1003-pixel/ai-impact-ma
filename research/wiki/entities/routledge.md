# Routledge

**Major academic publisher (imprint of Taylor & Francis / Informa); reprint publisher of Howson's due diligence textbook in 2017.**

## Overview

Routledge is an academic and professional publisher and an imprint of the Taylor & Francis Group (an Informa business), headquartered in London and New York. It reissued Peter Howson's *Due Diligence: The Critical Stage in Mergers and Acquisitions* in 2017, originally published by [[gower-publishing]] in 2003. [[2026-05-07-howson-2003-due-diligence-critical-stage]]

## Key facts

- Reprint publisher of Howson (2003/2017), ISBN 978-0-566-08524-6. [[2026-05-07-howson-2003-due-diligence-critical-stage]]
- Imprint of Taylor & Francis Group, an Informa business.
- Absorbed [[gower-publishing]] as an imprint.
