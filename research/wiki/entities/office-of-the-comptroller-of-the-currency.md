# Office of the Comptroller of the Currency

**U.S. federal agency within the Treasury Department that charters, regulates, and supervises national banks and federal savings associations; in 2018 announced the Fintech Charter Decision allowing nondepository fintech firms to apply for Special Purpose National Bank charters.**

## Overview

The OCC is the primary federal banking regulator for nationally chartered banks. Its enabling statute is the National Bank Act (NBA, 12 U.S.C. §§ 21–26), which authorises it to issue federal bank charters and prescribe associated regulations. In 2003 the OCC amended its charter regulations to create a path for Special Purpose National Bank (SPNB) charters for nondepository firms — the "2003 Amendments." [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]

On July 31, 2018, the OCC announced the Fintech Charter Decision: nondepository fintech firms could apply for SPNB charters, permitting them to operate in many respects as national banks. Johnson et al. (2019) argue this was the first attempt in the agency's 140-year history to regulate nondepository institutions as "banks" and that it exceeded the OCC's statutory authority. The New York DFS and the Conference of State Bank Supervisors (CSBS) filed federal lawsuits seeking to enjoin the OCC from issuing such charters. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]

The authors contend the Fintech Charter Decision replicates the OCC's pre-financial-crisis pattern of aggressively expanding its interpretive authority — previously over derivatives, now over fintech — with predictably harmful consequences for consumer protection and financial stability. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]

## Key facts

- Issued the Fintech Charter Decision on July 31, 2018, allowing fintech firms to apply for SPNB charters (12 C.F.R. § 5.20(e)(1)). [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
- The 2003 Amendments created the SPNB charter path for nondepository firms engaged in the "business of banking." [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
- The Fintech Charter Decision is the first attempt in OCC's 140-year history to charter nondepository institutions as national banks. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
- SPNB charters potentially preempt state antidiscrimination, consumer-protection, interest-rate cap, usury, and licensing laws. [[2026-05-07-johnson-pasquale-chapman-2019-ai-ml-bias-finance]]
