# KU Leuven

**Major Belgian research university in Leuven; institutional affiliation of Maarten Herbosch (Faculty of Law and Criminology), co-author of the 2025 AI risk allocation in M&A working paper.**

## Overview

KU Leuven (Katholieke Universiteit Leuven) is one of the oldest and largest universities in Belgium, consistently ranked among the leading research universities in Europe. Its Faculty of Law and Criminology is a significant centre for comparative private law, corporate law, and technology law research in the Belgian academic tradition. [[2026-05-04-herbosch-mertens-2025-risk-allocation-ai-ma]]

The Faculty is the institutional home of Maarten Herbosch, whose research on AI and private law — including the 2025 SSRN working paper on AI risk allocation in M&A — represents KU Leuven's contribution to the emerging field of AI law in transactional contexts. [[2026-05-04-herbosch-mertens-2025-risk-allocation-ai-ma]]
