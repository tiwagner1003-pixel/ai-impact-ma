# Daniel Greene

**Finance professor at the Wilbur O. and Ann Powers College of Business, Clemson University; co-author of the 2026 AI deal-screening paper and prior M&A and ChatGPT research.**

## Overview

Daniel Greene is a finance professor at the [[clemson-university|Wilbur O. and Ann Powers College of Business, Clemson University]]. He co-authored "Better Than a Coin Flip? Screening Mergers and Acquisitions With Artificial Intelligence Models" (SSRN 5421737, March 2026) with [[adam-bozman]] and [[douglas-fairhurst]]. [[2026-05-04-bozman-et-al-2026-ai-deal-screening]]

Greene has prior expertise in M&A research, including a 2016 paper on the wealth of private firm owners following reverse mergers (*Journal of Corporate Finance*, 37, 56–75). He also co-authored "How Much Does ChatGPT Know about Finance?" with Fairhurst (2025, *Financial Analysts Journal*, 81, 12–32), which established the baseline prompting methodology used in the AI deal-screening paper. The M&A deal sample construction in the 2026 paper uses Greene's own SPAC-filtering methodology developed in Greene (2016). [[2026-05-04-bozman-et-al-2026-ai-deal-screening]]
