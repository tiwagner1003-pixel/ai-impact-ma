# Journal of Management

**Peer-reviewed management journal that has published key M&A and information-asymmetry research, including Haleblian et al. (2009) and Bergh et al. (2019).**

## Overview

The *Journal of Management* (JOM) is one of the six leading management journals surveyed by Bergh et al. (2019) in their systematic review of information asymmetry research. It contributed 13 of the 223 reviewed articles. [[2026-05-07-bergh-et-al-2019-information-asymmetry-management-research]]

The wiki references JOM as the publication venue for Haleblian et al.'s M&A review [[2026-05-05-haleblian-et-al-2009-ma-review]] and for Bergh, Ketchen, Orlandi, Heugens, and Boyd (2019), "Information Asymmetry in Management Research: Past Accomplishments and Future Opportunities" (Vol. 45, No. 1, pp. 122–158, DOI: 10.1177/0149206318798026).
