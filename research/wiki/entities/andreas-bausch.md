# Andreas Bausch

**Professor of Strategic Management at Friedrich Schiller University Jena (Germany); senior author and likely corresponding author of Brede et al. (2025) on organizational cultural distance and M&A outcomes.**

## Overview

Andreas Bausch is the fourth (and likely most senior) author of "Mind the gap: the effect of cultural distance on mergers and acquisitions — evidence from Glassdoor reviews," published in the *Review of Managerial Science* (Vol. 19, 2025). He is affiliated with Friedrich Schiller University Jena. [[2026-05-04-brede-et-al-2025-cultural-distance-ma-glassdoor]]

Bausch is noted in the introduction as co-author on prior work cited in the paper (Dahlgrün and Bausch 2019, on organizational culture and outsourcing), suggesting established research in the intersection of strategy, organizational culture, and M&A. [[2026-05-04-brede-et-al-2025-cultural-distance-ma-glassdoor]]
