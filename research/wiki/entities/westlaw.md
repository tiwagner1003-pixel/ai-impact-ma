# Westlaw

**Thomson Reuters' legal research database, cited in Singh (2023) as an example of AI-powered natural language search enabling computers to comprehend how people think and use language.**

## Overview

Westlaw is a major legal research platform operated by Thomson Reuters. Singh (2023) cites it as a concrete example of AI applied in the legal field: AI-powered natural language processing in Westlaw enables more intuitive, language-based search, delivering faster and more logical results for legal professionals.
