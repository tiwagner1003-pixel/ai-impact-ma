# Javier Del Ser

**AI researcher at TECNALIA, University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), and the Basque Center for Applied Mathematics (BCAM); co-corresponding author of the 2020 XAI survey in *Information Fusion*.**

## Overview

Javier Del Ser holds multiple affiliations: TECNALIA (Derio, Spain), the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU, Bilbao), and the Basque Center for Applied Mathematics (BCAM, Bilbao). He is the co-corresponding author (alongside Alejandro Barredo Arrieta) of the landmark 2020 XAI survey. Del Ser's role per the CRediT statement was: Conceptualization, Investigation, Writing — original draft, Writing — review & editing, Visualization, Project administration, Supervision.
