# Ginni Rometty

**Former CEO of IBM, cited in Singh (2023) as having stated at a World Economic Forum panel that AI complements rather than replaces human capabilities.**

## Overview

Ginni Rometty (Virginia Marie Rometty) served as Chairman, President and CEO of IBM. Singh (2023) references her participation in a World Economic Forum panel alongside Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella, at which both executives emphasised that AI's role is to complement human capabilities rather than replace them — a key framing for the paper's argument that AI in M&A serves as an aid to human judgment.
