# Fannie Mae

**U.S. government-sponsored enterprise (GSE) that securitizes residential mortgages; its Loan Level Price Adjustment (LLPA) pricing grid (8×8 LTV/credit-score matrix) provides the identification strategy for Bartlett et al. (2022).**

## Overview

Fannie Mae (Federal National Mortgage Association) is one of the two U.S. government-sponsored enterprises (GSEs) that dominate the secondary mortgage market. Fannie Mae guarantees mortgages against default risk in return for guarantee fees set by its LLPA pricing grid. Because these fees are determined solely by LTV and credit score, any residual interest-rate variation within a grid cell cannot reflect differential credit risk — the identification strategy underpinning Bartlett et al. (2022). [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]

## Key facts

- LLPA pricing grid (8×8 LTV/credit-score matrix) is the primary identification lever in Bartlett et al. (2022). [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]
- GSEs (Fannie Mae + Freddie Mac) guarantee approximately 3.4 million of the 5.65 million loans in the core 2009–2015 Bartlett et al. sample. [[2026-05-07-bartlett-et-al-2022-consumer-lending-discrimination-fintech]]
