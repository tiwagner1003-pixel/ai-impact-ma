# Review of Managerial Science

**Peer-reviewed Springer management journal covering empirical and theoretical research across all management disciplines; publication venue for Brede et al. (2025) on cultural distance and M&A.**

## Overview

The *Review of Managerial Science* (RMS) is a peer-reviewed academic journal published by Springer. It covers empirical and theoretical research across all areas of management science, including strategic management, organizational behavior, and corporate finance. Brede et al. (2025) was published in Volume 19 (2025), pp. 2279–2326 (DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11846-024-00811-8). The paper was received 4 August 2023, accepted 10 September 2024, and published online 5 October 2024. [[2026-05-04-brede-et-al-2025-cultural-distance-ma-glassdoor]]
