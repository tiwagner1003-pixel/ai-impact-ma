# Lars Schweizer

**Professor of Management at Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt am Main, running the Bachelor seminar on M&A in SS 2026.**

## Overview

Prof. Dr. Lars Schweizer is a management academic at Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt. He leads the SS 2026 Bachelor seminar "Mergers & Acquisitions: Prozess und organisatorische Integration," covering the full deal lifecycle from M&A strategy and due diligence through post-merger integration and success measurement. [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]

His seminar framework organizes M&A knowledge into three layers: strategic context, the sequential transaction process, and outcome measurement — with AI and ESG as cross-cutting themes applied across all phases. [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]
