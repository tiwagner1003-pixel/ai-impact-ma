# Maarten Herbosch

**Assistant Professor of Artificial Intelligence and Law at KU Leuven, Faculty of Law and Criminology; leading Belgian legal scholar on AI liability, AI-assisted contracting, and AI use in M&A transactions.**

## Overview

Maarten Herbosch is based at the KU Leuven Faculty of Law and Criminology, where he specializes in the intersection of AI systems and private law, with a particular focus on contractual and liability issues arising from AI deployment. His research program spans AI liability, intelligent contracting, and the specific legal consequences of deploying AI tools in high-stakes transactional contexts such as M&A due diligence. [[2026-05-04-herbosch-mertens-2025-risk-allocation-ai-ma]]

His 2025 SSRN working paper with Floris Mertens provides one of the most comprehensive comparative legal analyses of AI risk allocation in M&A transactions, covering German, French, English, Belgian, and Delaware law. The paper is notable for applying both external liability doctrines (tool providers, service providers) and internal corporate governance doctrines (fiduciary duty, business judgment rule) to AI-guided M&A scenarios. [[2026-05-04-herbosch-mertens-2025-risk-allocation-ai-ma]]

Herbosch's prior solo publications cited extensively in the 2025 paper include: "Intelligent Contracteren" (Kluwer, 2023), "The Diligent Use of AI Systems: A Risk Worth Taking?" (EuCML 2022), "AI-Infused Contracting: An Application to M&A Transactions" (2024), and "Liability for AI Agents" (N.C. J.L. & Tech. 2025). Research funding from Fulbright and the Belgian American Educational Foundation. [[2026-05-04-herbosch-mertens-2025-risk-allocation-ai-ma]]
