# Julia A. Minson

**Behavioral scientist at Harvard Kennedy School studying advice-taking, disagreement, and collaborative judgment; co-author of the algorithm appreciation paper.**

## Overview

Julia A. Minson is a researcher at Harvard Kennedy School, Harvard University. Her work focuses on advice-taking behavior, negotiation, and the conditions under which people appropriately update their beliefs based on others' information. She is co-author, with Logg and Moore, of the 2019 algorithm appreciation study showing that lay people give more weight to algorithmically labeled advice than to identical human advice. [[2026-05-07-logg-minson-moore-2019-algorithm-appreciation]]

Prior relevant work includes the cost-of-collaboration literature (Minson & Mueller 2012 on how collective judgment amplifies rather than reduces disagreement) and the Liberman-Minson-Bryan-Ross (2012) wisdom-of-dyads paper.
