# Soenke Sievers

**Finance professor at Paderborn University and the sole academic author of the 2024 MASS paper; DFG/TRR 266 project member.**

## Overview

Soenke Sievers is a professor at Paderborn University (Warburger Str. 100, 33098 Paderborn) and the only non-BCG co-author on "Large Language Models and M&A: Can ChatGPT help forecast M&A activity?" (Degen et al. 2024). He acknowledges DFG/TRR 266 funding (Project-ID 403041268 — TRR 266 Accounting for Transparency) and declares no competing interests. [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]

His credited contributions span Writing, Supervision, Visualization, Conceptualization, Methodology, Validation, and Formal Analysis — suggesting he is the methodological and academic anchor for the paper's statistical and econometric rigor. [[2026-05-03-degen-et-al-2024-llms-ma-forecasting]]
