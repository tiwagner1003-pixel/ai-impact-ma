# Alejandro Lopez-Lira

**Finance professor at the University of Florida (Warrington College of Business); lead author of the 2025 paper formalizing the memorization non-identification problem for LLM-based economic forecasting.**

## Overview

Alejandro Lopez-Lira is a finance researcher at the University of Florida. His research sits at the intersection of empirical asset pricing and the application of machine learning and natural language processing to financial markets. He is co-author (with Yuehua Tang) of earlier work on LLMs and stock returns that intentionally restricts analysis to post-cutoff data to avoid the memorization problem — an approach endorsed as the standard solution in Lopez-Lira et al. (2025). [[2026-05-04-lopez-lira-et-al-2025-memorization-problem-llms]]

In Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu (2025), he develops the theoretical non-identification framework and leads the empirical testing program documenting systematic LLM memorization of economic and financial data. The paper is presented at the GSU-MS AI & FinTech Conference and the AMLED Webinar, and is submitted to the *Journal of Accounting, Auditing and Finance* (JAAF). [[2026-05-04-lopez-lira-et-al-2025-memorization-problem-llms]]
