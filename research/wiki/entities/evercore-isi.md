# Evercore ISI

**Independent investment banking advisory firm and equity research provider; co-author (with Professor David Shrier) of a report cited in Bremen (2024) concluding that AI will impact almost every job partially rather than eliminating most jobs.**

## Overview

Evercore ISI is the research and advisory division of Evercore, an independent investment bank. In Bremen (2024), an Evercore ISI report co-authored with Professor David Shrier is cited as evidence that AI primarily reshapes job roles rather than eliminating them — a claim Bremen uses to argue that AI in M&A will shift dealmakers toward higher-value activities rather than reducing M&A headcount. [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]

The specific report is not fully cited in Bremen (2024) — no title, date, or URL is provided. This limits the traceability of the claim for academic citation purposes.

## Key facts

- Co-authored a report with Professor David Shrier concluding that almost every job will be impacted by AI in some way, but only partially in most cases. [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]
- The report supports the framing of AI as a productivity enhancer and job-role shifter rather than a job replacer. [[2026-05-04-bremen-2024-ai-accelerates-ma]]
