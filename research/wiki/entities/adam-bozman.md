# Adam Bozman

**Finance researcher at the Carson College of Business, Washington State University; co-author of the 2026 working paper on AI-based M&A deal screening.**

## Overview

Adam Bozman is a finance researcher at the [[washington-state-university|Carson College of Business, Washington State University]]. He co-authored "Better Than a Coin Flip? Screening Mergers and Acquisitions With Artificial Intelligence Models" (SSRN 5421737, first draft April 2024, this draft March 2026) together with [[douglas-fairhurst]] and [[daniel-greene]]. [[2026-05-04-bozman-et-al-2026-ai-deal-screening]]

The paper represents an original empirical contribution applying both classical ML (ridge, lasso, random forests, gradient boosting) and LLMs (GPT-4) to the problem of forecasting investor reactions to M&A announcements, with a focus on out-of-sample performance and economic mechanisms (governance, complexity). Contact email per SSRN: adam.bozman@wsu.edu.
