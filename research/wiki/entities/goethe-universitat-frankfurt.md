# Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt am Main

**German public research university in Frankfurt, host institution of the SS 2026 Bachelor seminar on M&A.**

## Overview

Goethe-Universitat Frankfurt am Main is the university at which the SS 2026 Bachelor seminar "Mergers & Acquisitions: Prozess und organisatorische Integration" is held. Seminar sessions take place in room SH 3.105. [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]

## Key facts

- Seminar room: SH 3.105 [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]
- Course management platform: Moodle (used for scheduling consultation meetings) [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-intro]]
- Hosting department: Professur fur BWL, insb. Strategisches Management, RuW building, Theodor-W.-Adorno-Platz 4, 60323 Frankfurt [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-syllabus]]
- Library access for seminar readings: Goethe-Universitat online library and Business Source Premier database [[2026-05-03-bachelorseminar-ma-ss2026-syllabus]]

## Key facts

- Institutional AI policy: the university has published "Handlungsempfehlungen der Goethe-Universität zum Einsatz generativer KI," referenced as the governing document for AI use in seminar papers [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
- Chair for academic writing guidance: Marco Hochhaus (Lehrstuhl für BWL, insb. Strategisches Management) authored the SS 2026 scientific-writing introduction [[2026-05-03-einfuehrung-wiss-arbeiten]]
